Spring Outing

Spring g Outing Spring is here.

春游春天来了。

The weather is warm and the sky is blue.

天气暖和，天空湛蓝。

Our class is going on a spring outing today.

今天我们班去春游。

We are walking in the park.

我们在公园里散步。

The grass is green and the flowers are colourful.

草地绿油油的，花儿五彩缤纷。

Some birds are singing in the trees.

几只小鸟在树上唱歌。

We fly kites on the grass and have a picnic under a big tree.

我们在草地上放风筝,在一棵大树下野餐。

Everyone is laughing and playing.

每个人都笑着、玩着。

Spring is my favourite season.

春天是我最喜欢的季节。
