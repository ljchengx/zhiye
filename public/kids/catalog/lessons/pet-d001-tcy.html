World Data Organization Founded in Beijing

世界数据组织成立大会 Inaugural CeremonyoftheWorldDataOrganization WORLDDATA 中国·北京 BEIJING， CHINA2024.03.30 ORGANIZATION WORLD DATA ORGANIZATION WorldData Organization Founded in Beijing On March 30, the World Data Organization was officially set up in Beijing.

世界数据组织成立典礼 世界数据组织成立典礼 3月30日,世界数据组织在北京正式成立.

Representatives from more than 200 institutions took part in the opening ceremony.

来自200多个机构的代表参加了开幕式.

It aims to build open, fair rules for global data flow and digital cooperation.

其目的是为全球数据流动和数字合作制定公开、公平的规则。

Nowadays data has become a vital production resource.

现在,数据已成为重要的生产资源。

Without proper internationalrules, cross-borderdataactivitiesmayfacehiddenrisks.

如果没有适当的国际规则,跨国界数据活动可能会出现隐蔽风险。

The new organization offers a platform for members to talk about data safety, privacy protection and digital trade.

新组织为成员提供了一个讨论数据安全,隐私保护和数字贸易的平台.

Officials hope all member bodies can stick to mutual respect.

官员们希望所有成员机构都能坚持相互尊重.

By working together, they can reduce digital gaps between developed and developing countries and supportbalanced global digital development.

通过共同努力,它们可以缩小发达国家与发展中国家之间的数字鸿沟,支持平衡的全球数字发展。
