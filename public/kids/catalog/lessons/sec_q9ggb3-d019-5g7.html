What Is the Lantern Festival?

Read and Learn What Is the Lantern Festival?

阅读和学习 什么是灯光节?

The Lantern Festival is a happy Chinese holiday.

兰花节是快乐的中国节日.

It comes on the fifteenth day of the first lunar month.

初月十五日来.

This year, it is in February or March.

今年是2月或3月。

It is the end of the Spring Festival.

春节结束

People hang many red lanterns in the streets.

人们在街上挂着许多红灯笼.

Some lanterns have riddles on them.

有些灯笼上有谜语

Children love to guess the riddles.

孩子们都喜欢猜谜语

It is a fun game for the whole family.

这是整个家庭的游戏。

On this day, people eat tangyuan.

这天,人们会吃甘瓜

Tangyuan are small round balls made of rice.

唐 ⁇ 是小圆球,由米制成.

They are sweet and soft.

它们是甜的和柔软的。

Eating tangyuan means the family is together and happy.

吃甘露表示家人在一起快乐.

The Lantern Festival is a time for family.

灯神节是一家人的日子.

People watch the bright moon and lanterns at night.

人夜看明月灯.

They wish for good luck in the new year.

他们祝愿新年好运。

It is a beautiful and warm festival.

这是一个美丽和温暖的节日。
