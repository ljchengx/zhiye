Why Do We Have Many Dreams?

Why We Have Many Do you often have many dreams at night?

核心单词

Sometimes Is lots of dreaming equal to bad sleep?

有时梦境会和睡眠一样?

Do Dreams?

做梦?

you may dream about playing games, going to the park, or even strange things.

你可能梦想着玩游戏,去公园,甚至奇怪的事情。

Many kids think: “If I dream a lot, I don't sleep well." Is that true?

许多孩子认为:“如果我做梦很多,我睡不着。”这是真的吗?

Our brain never stops working, even when we are sleeping.

我们的大脑从不停止工作, 即使我们睡觉。

When we fall asleep, our body rests.

当我们睡着了, 我们的身体休息。

But part of our brain is still busy.

但我们的部分大脑仍然忙碌。

This is when dreams come.

这是梦来的时候。

Most people have 3-5 dreams every night.

大多数人每晚都有3-5个梦.

We usually forget most dreams when we wake up.

我们通常会忘记大多数的梦想 当我们醒来。

If you remember many dreams, it does not mean you sleep badly.

如果你记得很多梦想, 这并不意味着你睡得不好。

You just wake up during the dreaming time.

你只是在做梦的时候醒来的

What makes us dream more?

是什么让我们做更多的梦?

Think a lot during the day.

白天多想点

If you feel excited, worried or busy at school, your brain may make more dreams at night.

如果你感到兴奋,担心或在学校忙碌,你的大脑可能会在晚上做更多的梦.

Toomuchscreen time before bed.

床前的太过屏风时间

Looking at phones or tablets canmakeourbrainactive.

看着手机或平板电脑可以让大脑活跃起来

Then we have more dreams.

那我们还有更多的梦想。
