Do Dreams?

you may dream about playing games, going to the park, or even strange things.

Many kids think: “If I dream a lot, I don't sleep well." Is that true?

Our brain never stops working, even when we are

sleeping.

When we fall asleep, our body rests.

But part of our brain is still busy.

This is when dreams come.

Most people have 3-5 dreams every night.

We usually forget most dreams when we wake up.

If you remember many dreams, it does not mean you sleep badly.

You just wake up during the dreaming time.

What makes us dream more?

Think a lot during the day.

If you feel excited, worried or busy at school, your brain may make more dreams at night.
