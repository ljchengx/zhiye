Forests Green Lungs

Page 39

森林是大片林木生长的区域，被称为地球的“绿色之肺”。

Forests are large areas full of tall trees.

树木吸收有害气体，制造供我们呼吸的新鲜氧气，让空气变得清洁。

They are called “green lungs" of the Earth.

鸟类和许多小动物都在森林里安家。

Trees take in bad gas and make fresh oxygen for us to breathe.

森林还能保护水土、涵养水源，我们要好好爱护森林。

Forests keep the air clean.

别称：地球绿色之肺 1.单词默写：forest作用：净化空气、制造氧气、为动物提供家园、保持水土

Many birds and small animals make their homes in forests.

Forests also protect the land and bring us fresh water.

We need to protect forests every day.
