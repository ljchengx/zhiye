Whales Big Sea Mammals

English Text

鲸鱼是地球上体型最大的动物，属于大型海洋哺乳动物。

Whales are the biggest animals living on Earth.

鲸鱼必须呼吸空气，所以会经常游到海面换气。

They are large sea mammals.

呼吸时会喷出高高的水柱。

Whales must breathe air, so they often come to the sea surface.

不同种类的鲸鱼食物不同，有的捕食小鱼，有的以微型海洋生物为食。

They blow out waterspoutswhentheybreathe.

Different whales eat different food.

Some eat small fish, and some eat tiny sea life.
