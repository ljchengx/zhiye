Poo! Where Do You Go?

Poo!

粑粑，你去哪儿了？

Where Do You Go?

你按下马桶冲水按钮的时候，有没有想过一水都去哪儿了？

Have you ever flushed the toilet and wondered — where does the water go?

它踏上了一段长长的、秘密的旅程！

It goes on a long, secret journey!

你按下冲水键，水和粑粑一起流进一根管道里。

When you push the flush button, the water and poo go down a pipe.

管道把它们带出了你的家。

The pipe takes them out of your house.

接着，水流进街道下面更大的管道里。

Now the water travels through bigger pipes under the street.

这些管道把水送到一个叫“污水处理厂”的地方。

These pipes carry the water to a place called a water treatment plant.

那是一个专门清洗脏水的工厂。

That is a special factory that cleans dirty water.

在厂里，水要经过好多道工序。

At the plant, the water goes through many steps.

首先，大过滤器会拦住所有大块的东西。

First, big filters catch all the big stuff.

接着水在一个大池子里静置，让微小颗粒沉到底部。

Then the water sits in a big tank so the tiny bits sink to the bottom.

最后，水用特殊的化学物质和空气做深度清洁。

Finally, the water is cleaned with special chemicals and air.

水又变干净了！

It becomes clean again!

After all this cleaning, the water goes back to nature.

It flows into rivers or the sea.

Sometimes it is even used again!
