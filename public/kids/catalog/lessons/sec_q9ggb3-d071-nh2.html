科普启蒙篇 · 第 72 节

Bees Make Honey

English Text

蜜蜂是勤劳的群居昆虫，成千上万只蜜蜂共同生活、劳作在蜂蜡或木质的蜂巢里，每一只蜜蜂在族群中都有专属分工。

Bees are hard-working social insects.

工蜂会飞出蜂巢，在花丛中穿梭飞舞，采集花朵里香甜的花蜜，再将花蜜运回巢穴。

Thousands of beeslive and work together in a wooden or wax hive.

回到蜂巢后，蜜蜂们齐心协力，把新鲜的花蜜酿成美味的蜂蜜。

Every bee has its own job in the big family.

在采蜜的过程中，蜜蜂还会将花粉在花朵之间传递，帮助植物结果、结籽，对大自然益处良多。

Worker bees fly far away from the hive and dance among different flowers.

花粉传递习性：群居昆虫，分工协作作用：帮助植物传粉

They collect sweet nectar inside the flowers and carry it back home.

Inside the hive, bees work together to turn fresh nectar into tasty honey.

While collecting nectar, bees also carry pollen from one flower to another.

This helps flowers grow fruits and seeds.

Bees arevery helpful to nature.
