Chopstick Culture

Chopstick Culture

筷子是世界上最古老的进食工具之一，已有三千多年的历史。

Read and Learn

很久以前，古代中国人用树枝从热锅中夹取食物。

Chopsticks are one of the oldest eating tools in the world.

后来，筷子成为数亿人每天使用的工具。

They have a history of more than 3,000 years.

大多数筷子由竹子或木头制成，也有一些是塑料、金属甚至象牙做的。

Long ago, ancient Chinese people used sticks to pick up food from hot pots.

使用时，下面的筷子保持不动，上面的筷子像小杠杆一样活动。

Later, chopsticks became a daily tool for millions of people.

多加练习，你就能夹起米饭、面条，甚至一颗碗豆！

Most chopsticks are made of bamboo or wood.

筷子有很多礼仪规矩。

Some are made of plastic, metal, or even ivory.

不要把筷子竖直插在米饭里一一这看起来像葬礼上的香。

To use them, hold the lower stick still and move the upper stick like a small lever.

不要用筷子指着别人，也不要用筷子敲碗。

With practice, you can pick up rice, noodles, and even a single pea!

在中国、日本、韩国和越南，人们每天都用筷子，但每个国家的筷子风格不同。

Chopsticks come with rules.

比如，韩国筷子常常是金属的，而中国筷子更长。

Never stick them upright in your rice——-this looks like incense at a funeral.

学习筷子文化不只是学会吃饭，更是学会尊重、耐心和传统。

Don't point at people with them, and never use them to hit a bowl.

所以下次拿起筷子时，请记住：你手里握着的是一段历史。

In China, Japan, Korea, and Vietnam, chopsticks are used every day, but each country has its own style.

For example, Korean chopsticks are often metal, while Chinese ones are longer.

Learning chopstick culture is not just about eating——it is about respect, patience, and tradition.

So next time you pick up your chopsticks, remember: you are holding a piece of history.
