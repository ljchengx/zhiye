Fossils Old Animal Remains

English Text

化石是什么

Fossils are the old remains of ancient plants and animals.

化石是远古动植物的遗迹，历经数百万年慢慢变成了石头。

They turn into stone after millions of years.

很久很久以前，动物死亡后被泥沙、岩石掩埋，躯体逐渐石化，最终形成化石。

Long long ago, animals died and were covered by mud and rock.

科学家通过研究化石，了解远古生物的样貌与生活。

Slowly, their bodies changed into hard fossils.

形成：生物被掩埋→漫长岁月石化 2.英译中：ancient用途：帮助人类研究古生物

Scientists study fossils to learn what old animals looked like long ago.
