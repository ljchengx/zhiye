Whales Are Not Fish?

鲸鱼不是鱼？

Have you ever seen a whale in the ocean?

你在海洋里见过鲸鱼吗？

It looks like a big fish.

它看起来像一条大鱼。

But whales are not fish at all.

但鲸鱼根本不是鱼。

They are mammals, just like dogs, cats and people!

它们是哺乳动物，就像狗、猫和人一样！

Fish have scales on their bodies.

鱼身上长着鳞片。

Whales have smooth skin.

鲸鱼有光滑的皮肤。

Fish breathe with gills.

鱼用鳃呼吸，从水中获取氧气。

They take oxygen from the water.

鲸鱼用肺呼吸，就像我们一样。

Whales breathe with lungs, just like us.

它们必须浮到水面来呼吸空气。

They must come up to the surface to get air.

所以你经常可以看到鲸鱼从头顶的喷气孔中喷出水柱。

That is why you can see a whale spout water from its blowhole.

鱼产卵。

有些鲸鱼甚至会唱歌。
