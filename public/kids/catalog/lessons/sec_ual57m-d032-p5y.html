A Snowy Day

A Snowy Day

下雪天

It is snowing today.

今天下雪了。

Everything outside is white.

外面的一切都变白了。

The houses, the trees and the roads are all covered with snow.

房子、树木和道路都盖着雪。

My friends and I are playing in the snow.

我和朋友们正在雪地里玩。

We are making a big snowman.

我们在堆一个大大的雪人，它有红红的鼻子和两只黑眼睛。

It has a red nose and two black eyes.

一些男孩在打雪仗，一些女孩在雪地上画画。

Some boys are having a snowball fight.

天气很冷，但我们的心是暖的。

Some girls are drawing pictures on the snow.

It is cold, but our hearts are warm.
