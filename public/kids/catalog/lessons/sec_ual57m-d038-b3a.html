Riding a Bike

Riding a Bike Last week, my dad taught me to ride a bike.

学骑自行车上周，爸爸教我骑自行车。

At first, I was afraid of falling down.

一开始，我害怕摔倒。

Dad held the bike and said, "Look forward and keep going!" I tried again and again.

爸爸扶着车说：“看前方，继续骑！

Now I can ride by myself!

”我试了一次又一次。

Every weekend, I ride my bike in the park.

现在我可以自己骑了！

The wind blows on my face.

每个周末，我都在公园里骑车。

I ring the little bell: ding, ding!

风吹在我的脸上，我按响小铃铛：叮，叮！

Riding a bike is easy and fun.

骑自行车既简单又有趣。

I love my bike.

我爱我的自行车。
