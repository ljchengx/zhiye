Weekend Fun

Weekend Fun

快乐的周末

I love weekends because I can do many fun things.

我喜欢周末，因为我可以做许多有趣的事。

On Saturday morning, I play football with my friends in the park.

星期六上午，我和朋友们在公园里踢足球。

In the afternoon, I visit my grandparents.

下午，我去看望爷爷奶奶，奶奶给我们做可口的饭菜。

Grandma cooks delicious food for us.

星期天，我去图书馆读故事书。

On Sunday, I go to the library and read storybooks.

有时我和爸爸妈妈一起看场电影。

Sometimes I watch a film with my parents.

晚上，我们晚饭后散步。

In the evening, we take a walk after dinner.

周末虽然短暂，却让我快乐又放松。

Weekends are short, but they make me happy and relaxed.
