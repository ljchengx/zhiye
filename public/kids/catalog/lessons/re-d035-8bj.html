What Are the Chinese Zodiac Signs?

Read and Learn 十二生肖 What Are the Chinese Zodiac Signs?

中国黄道标志是什么?

The Chinese Zodiac has twelve animals.

中国黄道十二种动物.

They are Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig.

它们是:老鼠、牛、虎、兔子、龙、蛇、马、山羊、猴子、蟑螂、狗和猪。

Each year has one animal.

每年有一只动物。

Your birth year gives you that animal.

你出生的一年让你有这种动物。

Why are the animals in this order?

为什么动物要这样排列?

There is an old story.

有个老故事

The Jade Emperor held a race across a river.

玉帝驾车过江.

The Rat rode on the Ox and jumped off first.

老鼠骑着牛,先跳下去.

So the Rat is first, and theOxis second.

故鼠为先,奥克西为次.

Theother animals came one by one.

其他的动物一个接一个地来了

Your zodiac animal is very important in Chinese culture.

你的黄道动物在中国文化中非常重要.

People say it can show your character.

人们说它可以显示你的性格。

For example, Dragons are strong and brave.

譬如龙王坚固勇猛.

Rabbits are kind and quiet.

兔儿善和静.

Many people like to know their animal and its meaning.

很多人都喜欢了解他们的动物及其意义.

The zodiac also helps with age and time.

黄道也帮助年龄和时间.

It repeats every twelve years.

每十二年重复一次.

So people born twelve years apart share the same animal.

所以,人们出生 12年分开 分享相同的动物。

It is a fun way to learn about history and family.

了解历史和家庭是一种有趣的方式。
