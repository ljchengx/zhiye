火山喷发原理

A volcano is a special mountain.

火山是一种特殊的山体，山体内部有滚烫的熔融岩石，也就是岩浆。

Inside it, there is very hot melted rock called lava.

当地球内部压力不断累积，火山就可能发生喷发。

When pressure builds up inside the Earth, the volcano may erupt.

炽热的岩浆、火焰和浓烟会从火山口喷涌而出。

Hot lava, fire and thick smoke rush out from the top.

火山喷发威力巨大，十分危险，也有很多火山常年处于平静状态，不会喷发。

Volcano eruptions are powerful and dangerous.

Some volcanoes stay quiet for many years and never erupt.
