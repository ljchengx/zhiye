The Sun and Moon

The Sun and Moon

太阳和月亮

In the day, I see the sun.

白天，我看见太阳。

It is big and round.

它又大又圆。

It is Warm.

它暖暖的。

At night, I see the moon.

夜晚，我看见月亮。

It is bright.

它亮亮的。

The stars are small.

星星小小的。

Sun in the day, moon at night.

白天有太阳，夜晚有月亮。

How beautiful!

多么美丽啊！
