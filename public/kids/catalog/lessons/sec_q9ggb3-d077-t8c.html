Giraffes Long Neck

English Text

长颈鹿是陆地上最高的动物。

Giraffes are the tallest animals on land.

超长的脖子是它们最醒目的特征，让它们在草原上格外显眼。

Theirmost noticeable feature is theirincredibly long neck, which makes them stand out in grasslands.

依靠长长的脖子，长颈鹿能够轻松地到大树顶端鲜嫩的树叶和果实，而这些食物是草原上其他很多动物无法触及的。

Thanks to their long necks, giraffes can easily reach the tender leaves and fresh fruits on the top of tall trees.

长颈鹿全身长着漂亮的斑纹，步态从容，性情温顺安静。

These foods are too high for many other grassland animals to get.

它们通常小群活动，一天中的大部分时间都在采食树叶。

Giraffes have beautiful spotted fur all over their bodies.

They walk slowly and look gentle and quiet.

They usuallylive in small groups and spend most of their time eating leaves from trees.
