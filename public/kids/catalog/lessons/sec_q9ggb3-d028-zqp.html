Rainbow Science

English Text

雨后常常能见到彩虹，一道漂亮的彩虹包含七种色彩：红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、靛、紫。

We usually see rainbows after rain.

彩虹是奇妙的光学现象。

A beautiful rainbow indigo and violet.

阳光穿过空中的小雨滴时，光线发生折射、分解，最终变成七种颜色，彩虹就此出现。

Rainbows are a wonderful light phenomenon.

只有当太阳位于我们身后时，才能看到彩虹。

When sunlight passes through small raindrops in the air, the light bends and splits into seven different colors.

彩虹并不是实体，当空中水滴消散时，它也会随之消失。

This is how a rainbow comes out.

We can only see rainbows when the sun is behind us.

Rainbow is not a real thing.

It will disappear soon when the raindrops are gone.
