Coral Reefs - Ocean Gardens

English Text

★ 珊瑚礁是海底色彩斑斓的礁石，被人们称作“海洋花园”。

Coral reefs are colorful rocks under the sea.

★ 珊瑚由微小的海洋生物日积月累形成，容式各种的鱼类和海洋生物都栖息在珊瑚礁周围★珊瑚礁美丽又珍贵，是海洋生物重要的家园，我们要用心保护。

They are called “ocean gardens".

本质：由微小海洋生物形成作用：海洋生物栖息地 英译中：colorful

Corals are made by tiny sea animals.

Many kinds of fish and sea creatures live aroundcoralreefs.

Coral reefs are beautiful and important homes for ocean life.

We need to protect them.
