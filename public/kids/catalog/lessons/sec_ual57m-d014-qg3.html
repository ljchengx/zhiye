Happy Birthday

生日快乐

Today is my birthday.

今天是我的生日。

I am six.

我六岁了。

Look at the cake.

看这个蛋糕。

It is big.

它真大。

Six candles are on it.

上面有六根蜡烛。

Make a wish!

许个愿吧！

Blow!

吹蜡烛！

One, two, three!

一、二、三！

Happy birthday to me!

祝我生日快乐！
