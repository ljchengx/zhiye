Why Are Tree Trunks Painted White?

你见过树干被涂成白色的树吗？

Have you ever seen trees with white trunks?

它们看起来很好看，但背后有一个很重要的原因。

They look nice, but there is a very good reason for it.

冬天，阳光依然很明亮。

In winter, the sun can still be bright.

深色的树皮会吸收阳光，白天变暖。

Dark tree bark absorbs sunlight and gets warm during the day.

到了晚上，温度迅速下降，树皮又变冷。

At night, the temperature drops quickly, and the bark becomes cold again.

这种巨大的温差会让树皮开裂。

This big change can make the bark crack.

白色的涂料能反射阳光，让树干在白天保持较凉的温度。

White paint reflects sunlight, so the trunk stays cooler during the day.

这样温差就变小了，树皮也就安全了。

Then the temperature change is smaller, and the bark stays safe.

这种白色涂料不是普通的油漆，通常由石灰、盐，有时还有硫磺制成。

The white paint is not just ordinary paint.

这种混合物可以杀死树皮上的小细菌和昆虫，还能阻止一些虫子爬上树。

It is usually made with lime, salt, and sometimes sulfur.

所以，白色涂料就像树木的防晒霜和外套，让它们保持健康和安全。

This mixture can kill small germs and insects on the bark.

这种混合物可以杀死树皮上的小型细菌和昆虫.

It also stops treesforhundreds of years!

百年的树也都停了!

some bugs from climbing up the tree.

爬上树上的虫子

So, white paint is like a sunscreen and a coat for trees.

故白漆如防晒霜,为树木涂衣.

It keeps them healthy and safe.
