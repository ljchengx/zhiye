On the Train

On the Train

在火车上

Last week, I took a train to Shanghai with my mother.

上周，我和妈妈坐火车去上海。

It was my first long train journey, so I was very excited.

这是我第一次长途火车旅行，所以我非常兴奋。

The train was fast and clean.

火车又快又干净。

I sat by the window and watched the world outside.

我坐在窗边，看着外面的世界。

Green fields, small houses and white clouds ran past us.

绿色的田野、小小的房屋和白云从我们身旁掠过。

A kind old man sat next to me.

一位和蔼的老人坐在我旁边，他给我讲了沿途城市的有趣故事。

He told me interesting stories about the cities along the way.

后来，一位热情的女士在火车上卖水果和饮料，妈妈给我买了一盒草莓，吃起来甜甜的。

Later, a nice lady sold fruit and drinks on the train.

四个小时后，火车到达了上海。

Mum bought me a box of strawberries.

多么愉快的旅程啊！

They tasted sweet.

After fourhours, the train arrived inShanghai.

What a pleasant journey!
