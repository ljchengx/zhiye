Yummy Apples

好吃的苹果

I like apples.

我喜欢苹果。

They are red.

它们红红的。

They are sweet.

它们甜甜的。

One apple, two apples!

一个苹果，两个苹果！

Wash the apple.

Bite it.

Yummy, yummy!

真好吃，真好吃！

An apple a day, I am happy.

一天一个苹果，我开开心心。
