The School Show

The School Show

学校演出

Today our school is having a big show in the hall.

今天我们学校在大礼堂举行一场盛大的演出。

Many students are performing on the stage.

许多学生正在舞台上表演。

Lily is singing an English song.

莉莉在唱一首英文歌，她的歌声很甜美。

Her voice is sweet.

一些男孩随着音乐跳舞，两个女孩在一起弹钢琴。

Some boys are dancing to the music.

我们班正在表演一个关于聪明兔子的短剧，我就是那只兔子！

Two girls are playing the piano together.

大家热烈鼓掌，老师们笑容满面。

My class is acting a short play about a clever rabbit.

我们刻苦排练，演出获得了巨大成功。

I am the rabbit!

Everyone claps their hands.

Teachers are smiling.

We practice hard, and the show is a great success.
