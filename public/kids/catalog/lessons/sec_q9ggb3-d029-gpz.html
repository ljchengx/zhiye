科普启蒙篇 · 第 30 节

Sunrise and Sunset

English Text

每天清晨，太阳从东方地平线升起；傍晚，又落向西方地平线，这就是日出与日落。

Every day, the sun rises from the east horizon in the morning and sets down to the west horizon in the evening.

看似太阳在天空移动，实际上是地球在不停自转。

This is sunrise and sunset.

随着地球转动，地球上不同区域依次被阳光照亮。

The sun seems to move across the sky, but in fact, it is the Earth that spins all the time.

日出带来光明，开启崭新的一天；日落将天空染成金红色，夜幕降临，世界也慢慢安静下来。

As Earth turns, different places get sunlight one after another.

科学原理：视觉上的太阳移动，本质是地球自转。

Sunrise brings bright daylight and starts a new day.

景象特点：日出喷薄日色，日落晚霞绚丽。

Sunset paints the sky with golden and red colors.

2. 英译中：sunset

The world turns quiet as night comes.
