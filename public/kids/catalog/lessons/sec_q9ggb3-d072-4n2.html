Ants Live in Groups

English Text

蚂蚁是地球上最勤劳的昆虫之一。

Ants are one of the most hard-working insects on Earth.

它们从不独居，总是在地下组成庞大的蚁群共同生活。

They never live alone and always form big groups called ant colonies underground.

蚂蚁用头部和四肢在泥土中挖掘深深的地道和小洞穴，这片地下空间就是它们温暖又安全的家。

Ants use their heads and legs to dig deep tunnels and small rooms in the soil.

蚁群分工明确：有的出去觅食，有的照料幼蚁，还有的负责守卫巢穴。

These underground spaces are theirwarm and safe homes.

令人惊讶的是，小小的蚂蚁却有惊人的力量，能够搬运比自身重数倍的食物和物品。

Different ants take different jobs: some look for food, some take care of baby ants, and some guard the nest.

面对繁重的工作，它们总会团队协作，共同完成。

Surprisingly, tiny ants have amazing strength.

习性：群居、地下筑巢、分工协作 单词默写：ant特点：体型小、力气大、团队意识强 判断：Ants live alone.(T / F)

They can carry food and objects that are many times heavier than their own bodies.

They always work as a team to finish hard tasks.
