The Qipao: A Beautiful Chinese Dress

Read and Learn The Qipao: A Beautiful Chinese Dress The qipao, also called cheongsam, is a famous Chinese dress with a long history.

It started from the Manchu people hundreds of years ago.

Later, in the 1920s, dressmakers in Shanghai changed the design to make it more modern and stylish.

Now, the qipao is known all over the world.

What does a qipao look like?

It usually has a high collar, a fitted body, and side slits.

These features make women look elegant and graceful.

Traditional qipaos are made of silk or cotton, and they often have beautiful patterns like flowers, dragons, or birds.

Some are embroidered by hand, which takes a lot of time and skill.

Today, people wear qipaos on special occasions.

For example, women may wear them at weddings, Chinese New Year, or formal parties.

Many actresses wear qipaos on the red carpet.
