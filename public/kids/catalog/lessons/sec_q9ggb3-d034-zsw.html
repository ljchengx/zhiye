Autumn Harvest

Autumn is the harvest season.

秋天是收获的季节，气候凉爽宜人。

The weather becomes cool and comfortable.

秋季里，瓜果、蔬菜、粮食纷纷成熟，农民们采摘作物，迎来丰收，因此秋天也被称作“金色的季节”。

In autumn, fruits, vegetables and grains all get ripe.

气温慢慢转凉，树木不再需要大量叶片，树叶渐渐变黄、变红、变棕，纷纷飘落。

Farmers pick crops and get rich harvests.

落叶是秋日一道最美的风景。

That is why we call autumn the golden season.

是秋季标志性景象。

As the weather gets colder, trees do not need so many leaves.

Leaves turn yellow, red or brown and fall down to the ground.

Falling leaves are the most beautiful view of autumn.
