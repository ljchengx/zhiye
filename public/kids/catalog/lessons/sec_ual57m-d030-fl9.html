No Junk Food

Tom likes junk food very much.

不吃垃圾食品

He often eats chips,

hamburgers and candy.

汤姆非常喜欢吃垃圾食品。

He drinks cola every day.

他经常吃薯片、汉堡包和糖果，每天喝可乐。

Now he feels tired and his teeth hurt.

现在他觉得很累，牙齿也疼。

The doctor says, " Junk food is bad for your health.

医生说：“垃圾食品对你的健康有害。

Eat more fruit and vegetables.

多吃水果和蔬菜，喝水，不要喝可乐。

Drink water, not cola." Tom listens to the doctor.

”汤姆听了医生的话。

Now he eats an apple every day and plays sports after school.

现在他每天吃一个苹果，放学后还做运动。

He feels strong and happy again.

他又变得健康又快乐了。
