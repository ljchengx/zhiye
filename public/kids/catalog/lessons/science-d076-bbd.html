但这是不对的！

Have you ever seen a camel?

那骆驼的驼峰里到底装的是什么呢？

It has one or two big humps on its back.

骆驼的驼峰里装的其实是脂肪，就像一个背在身上的“食物背包”！

Many people think the humpsarefullofwater.

脂肪储存在驼峰里，等骆驼没有食物吃的时候，身体就会利用驼峰里的脂肪来提供能量。

Butthatisnottrue!

脂肪用完的时候，驼峰会变小、拉下来。

So what is inside a camel's hump?

等骆驼好好吃一顿之后，驼峰又会重新变大、变挺。

A camel'shumpisactuallyfulloffat.

那骆驼把水储存在哪里呢？

It is like abigbackpackoffood!

骆驼不把水储存在驼峰里。

Thefatisstored there so the camel can use it later for energy.

它们是一次喝很多水最多能喝40加仑！

When acameldoesnothavefood toeat,its body uses the fat in its hump.

差不多是满满一浴缸的水！

The hump can get smaller and floppy when the fat is used up.

它们的身体很擅长保存水分，不经常出汗，也不经常排尿。

When the camel eats well again, the hump becomes big and firm once more.

这让它们能在炎热的沙漠里生存下来。

But wait—where do camels store water?

>所以，骆驼的驼峰不是水箱一它是食物箱，是沙漠里自带的生存工具包！

Camels do not store water in their humps.

They drink a lot of water at one time — up to 40 gallons!

That is a whole bathtub full of water!

Their bodies are very good at holding onto the water.

They do not sweat much, and they do not pee very often.

This helps them survive in the hot desert.

So, a camel's hump is not a water tank.

It isafood tank—abuilt-insurvivalkitfor the desert！
