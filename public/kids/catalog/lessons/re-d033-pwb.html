What Is Peking Opera?

Read and Learn What Is Peking Opera?

阅读和学习什么是北京歌剧院?

Peking Opera is a famous kind of Chinese opera.

北京歌剧院是中国京剧的著名类型.

It started in Beijing about 200 years ago.

始于约200年前的北京.

It is called the "national treasure" of China.

其被称为中国的"国宝".

People all over the world love to watch it.

世间众生皆爱观.

There are four main roles in Peking Opera: Sheng, Dan, Jing, and Chou.

北京歌剧院主要有四部角色:盛,丹, ⁇ ,周.

Sheng is the male role, and Dan is the female role.

盛是男角色,丹是女角色.

Jing is the painted-face role, and Chou is the funny clown.

静是画脸的角色,周是滑稽的小丑.

Each role has its own special costume and style.

每个角色都有自己的特殊服装和风格.

The face paintings are very interesting.

面部画作非常有趣.

Red means brave and loyal, like Guan Yu.

红色意为勇敢而忠诚,如关羽.

White means clever but tricky, like Cao Cao.

The colors help the audience know the character quickly.

颜色帮助观众快速了解角色.

Performers use four skills: singing, speaking, acting, and fighting.

表演者使用四种技能:唱歌,说话,演技,以及战斗.

They also use beautiful movements and music.

他们也使用美丽的动作和音乐.
