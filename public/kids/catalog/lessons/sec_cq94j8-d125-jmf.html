Lesson 125 Tea for two

Tea for two

两个人一起喝茶

SUSAN Can't you come in and have tea now, Peter?

苏珊,你现在不能进来喝茶吗,彼得?

Not yet.

还没有

PETER I must water the garden first.

彼得,我必须先给花园浇水

SUSAN Do you have to water it now?

苏珊,你现在要浇水吗?

I'm afraid I must.

恐怕我必须这么做

PETER Look at it!

彼得,看这个!

It's terribly dry.

干得特别干燥

SUSAN What a nuisance!?

苏珊,你这讨厌鬼!

Last summer it was very dry, too.

去年夏天也非常干燥。

PETER Don't you remember?

彼得,你不记得了吗?

I had to water it every day.

我每天都得给它浇水

SUSAN Well, I'll have tea by myself.

苏珊,我一个人喝茶

SUSAN That was quick!

苏桑,那太快了!

Have you finished already?

说完了吗?

Yes.

对

PETER Look out of the window.

彼特从窗外看.

It's raining!

下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了! 下雨了!

5 SUSAN That means you don't need to water the garden.

5苏珊,这意味着你不需要给花园浇水。

PETER That was a pleasant surprise.

PETER,这是一个令人愉快的惊喜。

It means I can have tea, instead.

意思是我可以喝茶,换句话说。
