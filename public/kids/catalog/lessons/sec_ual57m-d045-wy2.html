Lost and Found

Lost and Found

失物招领

Yesterday I lost my blue watch on the playground.

昨天我在操场上弄丢了我的蓝色手表。

I was very sad because it was a gift from Grandpa.

我很难过，因为那是爷爷送我的礼物。

This morning, I went to the Lost and Found office.

今天早上，我去了失物招领处。

A kind teacher opened a box.

一位和谌的老师打开了一个盒子。

My watch was there!

我的手表就在里面！

A boy in Grade Five found it.

一个五年级的男孩捡到了它。

Being honest is a beautiful thing.

诚实是一件美好的事。
