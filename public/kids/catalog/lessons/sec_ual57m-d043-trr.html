Going to the Park

Going to the Park

去公园

This afternoon, our family is going to the city park.

今天下午，我们一家人去城市公园。

We take a bus there.

我们乘公交车到那里。

The park is big and beautiful.

公园又大又美，许多人玩得很开心。

Many people are having fun.

老人在湖边锻炼身体，一些孩子在草地上做游戏，一位年轻人正在给花儿拍照。

Old people are doing exercise near the lake.

我和哥哥租了一条小船在湖上划。

Some children are playing games on the grass.

湖水清澈，微风凉爽。

A young man is taking photos of the flowers.

我们都度过了一段美好的时光。

My brother and I rent a small boat and row on the lake.

The water is clean and the wind is cool.

We all have a wonderful time.
