By Bus or By Bike?

By Bus or By Bike?

坐公交还是骑车？

GOOD MORHING DOWNTOWN BOOKS CITYBUS My home is three kilometres from school.

我家离学校三公里。

Every morning, I think: by bus or by bike?

每天早上我都在想：坐公交还是骑车？

The bus is fast and comfortable.

公交车又快又舒服，我可以在车上看书，但有时很拥挤。

I can read on the bus.

自行车慢一点，却是很好的锻炼，我可以享受新鲜空气和早晨的阳光。

But sometimes it is crowded.

现在我有了一个计划：下雨天坐公交，晴天骑自行车。

The bike is slow, but it is good exercise.

两种方式都绿色环保。

I can enjoy the fresh air and the morning sunshine.

Now I have a plan.

On rainy days, I take the bus.

On sunny days, I ride my bike.

Both ways are green and good.
