Giant Pandas: China's Lovely Treasure

China'sLovely Giant Pandas: Treasure

大熊猫是体型大、圆滚滚的动物，长着黑白相间的毛。

Giant pandas are big, round animals with black and white fur.

它们有黑色的耳朵、黑色的眼圈、黑色的腿，还有白色的脸和肚子。

They have black ears, black eye patches, black legs, and a white face and belly.

它们的身体看起来像柔软的球，走路的样

Their bodies look like soft balls, and they walk in a slow, funny way.

Adult pandas can weigh up to 150 kilograms, but baby pandas are tiny and pink when they are born.

Pandas love to eat bamboo.

They spend about 12 hours a day eating, and they can eat 30kilograms of bamboo each day!

They live in cool, wet forests in the mountains of central China.

Pandas are usually alone, and they sleep in trees or in caves.

They are very good climbers, even though they look heavy.

Giant pandas are a symbol of China and are called "national treasures." They are gentle and peaceful, and people all over the world love them.

But pandas are endangered because their forest homes are shrinking.

Protecting pandas means protecting the forests, which also helps other animals and clean air.

By saving pandas,we aresavingnaturefor everyone.
