Can a Snake Find True Love?

Have you ever heard of a snake who fell in love with a human?

TheLegend of theWhiteSnakeis oneof China's four most famousfolk tales.

It tells the story of Bai Suzhen, a white snake spirit who turns into a beautiful woman.

She meets a young man named Xu Xian by the West Lake in Hangzhou.

Bai Suzhen and Xu Xian fall in love and get married.

But not everyone is happy for them.

A monk named Fahaibelieves that a snake and ahuman shouldnever be together.

He tries to separate them by trappingBai Suzhen under the Leifeng Pagoda.

The story has magic, rain, umbrellas, and a famous bridge called the Broken Bridge.

One of themost excitingscenes happens on the Broken Bridge.

Bai Suzhen and Xu Xian meet there in the rain, and Xu Xian lends her his umbrella.

That small act of kindness starts their love story.

Later, Bai Suzhen risks everything to save her husband, even fighting with magic against Fahai.

Her love is brave and strong.

Today, the legend is still loved by people of all ages.

You can see it in movies, TV shows, and even video games.

The story teaches us that love is powerful, and that we should not judge others bywhat theylook like.

Next time you visit Hangzhou, look for the Broken Bridge and the Leifeng Pagoda — and remember the white snake who loved deeply.
