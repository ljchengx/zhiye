Long ago, a boy named Mengzi lived with his mother.

At first, they lived near a cemetery.

Little Mengzi played funerals every day.

His mother said, “This is not a good place to learn."

They moved near a market.

Mengzi played selling things and shouting prices.

His mother moved again.

This time, they lived next to a school.

Mengzi watched students read books and bow to the teacher.

He began to study hard.

His mother smiled.

A good place and a caring family help a child grow.

中文翻译

很久以前，有个男孩叫孟（孟子），和母亲住在一起。

起初，他们住在墓地附近。

小孟子每天玩办丧事的游戏。

母亲说：“这不是学习的好地方。

”他们搬到了集市附近。

孟子学着叫卖东西、喊价钱。

母亲叉搬了家。

这次，他们住在学校旁边。

孟子看着学生们读书、向老师鞠躬。

他开始努力学习。

母亲笑了。

好的环境和关爱家人的家庭，能帮助孩子成长。
