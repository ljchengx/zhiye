Elephants Big Smart Animals

English Text

大象是地球上体型最大的陆地动物。

Elephants are the largest land animals on our planet.

它们身躯庞大，长着大大的耳朵、洁白的长牙，还有功能强大的长鼻子。

They have huge bodies, big ears, long white tusks and the most amazing long trunks.

象鼻是大象非常重要的工具，大象用鼻子取食、饮水、洗澡，甚至和同伴打招呼。

Their trunks arevery useful tools.

大象智商很高，记忆力出色，它们以大家族为单位群居生活。

Elephants use trunks topick up food,drink water,take a shower and evengreettheirfamilymembers.

族群里的大象会悉心照顾幼象，遇到困难时也会互相帮助。

Elephants are extremely clever animals.

大象大多栖息在森林与草原之中。

They have good memories and live together in big family groups.

They take good care of the young elephants and help each other when they meet trouble.

Most elephants live in forests and grasslands.
