Why Aren't Polar Bears?

北极熊生活在北极，那里非常非常冷。

Afraid of theCold?

但北极熊一点也不怕。

Polar bears live in the Arctic.

它们是怎么保暖的呢？

It is very, very cold there.

北极熊有两层毛。

But polar bears are not afraid.

外层挡住冰雪，内层锁住热气，让北极熊暖暖的。

How do they stay warm?

北极熊的皮肤下面有一层厚厚的脂肪，叫作“鲸脂”。

Polar bears have two layers of fur.

脂肪不仅保暖，还能在食物难找的时候提供能量。

The outer layer keeps snow and ice away.

北极熊的耳朵小小的，尾巴短短的，这样热量就不会跑掉。

The inner layer traps warm air close to the body.

告诉你一个惊喜一一北极熊的皮肤是黑色的！

This keeps the bear warm.

黑色能吸收太阳的热量。

Under the skin, polar bears have thickfatcalled blubber.

北极熊天生就是为寒冷而生的一一它的皮毛、脂肪和皮肤一起工作，让它暖暖和和的！

It keeps them warm and gives them energy when food is hard to find.

Polar bears have small ears and a short tail.

This stops heat from escaping.

Surprise!

A polar bear's skin is black.

Black absorbs heat from the sun.

Polar bears are built for the cold.

Their fur, fat, and skin all work together!
