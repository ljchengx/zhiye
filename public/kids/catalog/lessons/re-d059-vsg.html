Traditional

Chinese Medicine Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is a very old way of keeping people healthy.

It started more than 2,000 years ago in China.

TCM believes that our body has two forces: Yin and Yang.

When Yin and Yang are balanced, we are healthy.

When they are not, we may feel sick.

TCM uses many different methods to help people.

One is herbal medicine—-doctors use plants and roots to make special drinks or pills.

Another is acupuncture, which uses very thin needles.

The needles are put into the skin at certain points to help the body heal itself.

TCM also includes massage and special exercises like Tai Chi.

Today, TCM is famous all over the world.

Many countries have TCM clinics, and scientists are studying it more and more.

Some people use TCM togetherwithWestern medicine tofeel better.
