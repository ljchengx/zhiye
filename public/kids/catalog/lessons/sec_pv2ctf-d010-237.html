What is in your bedroom?

你的卧室里有什么？

There is a bed and a desk in my bedroom.

我的卧室里有一张床和一张书桌。

Where do you sleep?

你在哪里睡觉？

I sleep in bed.

我睡在床上。

I turn off the light and go to bed.

我关灯上床睡觉。
