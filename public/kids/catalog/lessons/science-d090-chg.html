There is no fire inside.

So how does it work?

The microwave sends out tiny waves of energy.

We cannot see them, but they go right into the food.

Inside the food there are tiny bits of water.

The waves make thesewaterbits shake very, very fast —— millions of times every second!

All this shaking makes heat, and the heat warms up the food.

That is why food with more water heats up faster.
