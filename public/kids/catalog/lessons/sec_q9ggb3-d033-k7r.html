科普启蒙篇 · 第 34 节

Spring Growth

English Text

春天是生机勃勃的季节，气温回暖，寒风渐渐退去。

Spring is a lively season.

温暖的气候与绵绵春雨唤醒了大自然：树木抽出新芽，长出绿叶，小草从泥土里钻出来，百色花朵次第绽放。

The weather turns warm, and cold wind is gone.

动物们也变得活跃，四处活动，觅食。

Warm temperature and soft rain wake up nature.

春天是万物生长、蓬勃繁衍的最佳时节。

Trees grow new buds and green leaves.

2.植物变化：抽芽、长叶、开花，一片绿意。

Grass comes out from the soil.

1.填空：Flowers in spring.

All kinds of flowers bloom one by one.

Animals also become active.

They walk around and look for food.

Spring is the best time for all living things to grow and thrive.
