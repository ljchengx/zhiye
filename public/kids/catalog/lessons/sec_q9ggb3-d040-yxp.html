Mountains Tall Lands

English Text

高山是高大又陡峭的地形，山的顶端叫做山顶。

Mountains are very tall and steep lands.

爬山爬得越高，空气就越寒冷。

The top of a mountain is called a peak.

许多高山山顶终年被积雪覆盖。

The higher we climb a mountain, the colder the air becomes.

山体由坚硬的岩石构成，这里生活着喜爱寒冷环境的动植物。

Many tall mountains are covered with snow all year round.

很多河流都发源于高山之上。

Mountains are made of hard rocks.

环境：海拔越高气温越低，高山常终年积雪

They have special plants and animals that like cold weather.

Many rivers start from high mountains.
