Up, Up, and Away! How Does an Elevator Work?

in Life Motor

（电机）

Up, Up, and Away!

电梯是怎么工作的？

How Does an?

（滑轮）

ElevatorWork?

（钢缆）

Have you been in a box that goes up and down in a tall building?

你坐过高楼里上上下下的“小箱子”吗？

That is an elevator!

那就是电梯！

Howdoesitwork?

它是怎么工作的呢？

A motor turns a wheel called a pulley.

电机带动一个叫滑轮的轮子。

A strong cable goes around the pulley and connects to the elevator car.

一根结实的钢缆绕过滑轮，连着电梯轿厢。

When the motor turns one way, the car goes up.

电机往一边转，轿厢就上去；往另一边转，轿厢就下来。

When it turns the other way, the car goes down.

电梯还有一个叫“对重块”的重物，它朝反方向移动，帮电机省力。

There is also a heavy weight called a Counterweight counterweight.

（对重块）

It moves the oppositeway to help the motor save energy.

电梯有很厉害的刹车。

Elevators have strong brakes.

如果钢缆断了，刹车能让轿厢停下来，不会掉下去。

If the cable breaks, the brakes stop the car from falling.

电梯门还有感应器。

The doors have sensors, too.

如果有东西挡在中间，门就不会关上。

If something is in the way, the doors will not close.

所以下次坐电梯的时候，你就知道啦一一是电机、滑轮、对重块和聪明的感应器一起工作，带你上上下下的！

So next time you ride an elevator, you will know - a motor, a pulley, a weight, and somesmartsensorsworktogether to take you up and down!

向上，向上，出发！
