Nobel Prizes

WORLDNEWS ABRIGHTERWORLDTHROUGHKNOWLEDGE The2026 GREAT PEOPLE Nobel Prizes SCIENCE BRIGHTER HUMANITY ALFRED TOMORROWS PEACE NOBEL SCIENCE 丨IDEAS IA BETTER WORLD The Nobel Prizes are world-famous awards.

They honour people who do great work for the world.

In early October, this year's Nobel Prizes were announced one by one.

OCTOBER5 Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel win Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel won the prize for creating optogenetics.

This amazing technology uses light to switch nerve cells in the brain PHYSIOLOGY on and off.

It helps uslearn more about how our brain works.

ORMEDICINE Key contribution: Optogenetics - using light to control nerve cells in the brain.

OCTOBER6 Francis Halzen wins Nobel Prize in Physics Francis Halzen won the prize for leading the building of the IceCube Neutrino Observatory.

It sits deep under the ice at the South Pole in Antarctica.

It detects tiny particles called neutrinos from deep space.

It helps us know more about our universe.

PHYSICS Key contribution: The IceCube Neutrino Observatory - detecting neutrinos from deep space.

OCTOBER7 Henri B.

Kagan and Kenso Soai win Nobel Prize in Chemistry Henri B.

Kagan and Kenso Soai won the prize for discovering non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.

Their work helps chemists make just one of a molecule's two mirror images.

We can use such molecules to create safer newmedicines.

CHEMISTRY Key contribution: Non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.

OCTOBER8 Anne Carson wins Nobel Prize in Literature Anne Carson, a Canadian poet and writer, won the prize.

She writes poems and storiesinboldnewways.

She alsotranslates old Greek andRomanworksintoEnglish.

Key contribution: Bold and original writing, and translations of Greek and Roman works.
