A New Friend

A New Friend Today we have a new classmate.

一位新朋友今天我们班来了一位新同学。

Her name is Lily.

她的名字叫莉莉。

She is from Shanghai.

她来自上海。

She has big eyes and a sweet smile.

她有一双大眼睛和甜甜的笑容。

She likes reading and drawing.

她喜欢读书和画画。

At break time, I say hello to her.

课间休息时，我向她问好。

We talk and play together.

我们一起聊天、玩要。

Now we are good friends.

现在我们成了好朋友。

I am very happy to have a new friend.

有了一位新朋友，我非常开心。
