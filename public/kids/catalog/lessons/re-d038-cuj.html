Qin Shi Huang: China's First Emperor

Qin Shi Huang: China's First Emperor Qin Shi Huang was the first emperor of a united China.

He lived over 2,200 years ago and was originally named Ying Zheng.

Before him, China was divided into seven warring states.

Ying Zheng was the king of the Qin state, and one by one, he conquered all the other states.

In 221 BC, he became the first person to rule all of China.

As emperor, Qin Shi Huang did many great things.

He standardised the writing system, so

people in different parts of China could understand the same characters.

He also standardised money, weights, measures, and even the width of roads.

He began building the Great Wall to protect China from northern enemies.

These changes helped turn separate kingdoms into one united country.

Qin Shi Huang also built a huge underground army to guard him in the afterlife.

This is the famous Terracotta Army, discovered near Xi'an in 1974.

Thousands of life-sized clay soldiers, horses, and chariots stand in rows underground.

Every soldier has a different face.

It is one of the most amazing discoveries in the world.

However, Qin Shi Huang was also a strict and

harsh ruler.

He burned books and punished people who disagreed with him.

After he died, his dynasty quickly fell apart.

Even so, Qin Shi Huang changed Chinese history forever.

The name "China" may even come from the word "Qin."

秦始皇是中国统一后的第一位皇帝。

他生活在两千两百多年前，原名赢政。

在他之前，中国分裂为七个战国。

赢政是国的国王，他逐一征服了所有其他国家。

公元前221年，他成为第一个统治全中国的人。

作为皇帝，秦始皇做了许多伟大的事。

他统一了文字，让中国不同地区的人们能看同样的汉字。

他还统一了货币、度量衡，甚至道路的宽度。

他开始修建长城来保护中国免受北方敌人的侵扰。

这些变化帮助把分的王国变成了一个统一的国家。

秦始皇还建造了一支巨大的地下军队来守护他的来世。

这就是著名的兵马俑，1974年在西安附近发现。

成千上万的真人大小的陶制士兵、陶马和战车排列在地下。

每个士兵都有不同的面容。

这是世界上最令人叹的发现之一。

然而，秦始皇也是一位严酷的统治者。

他书坑，惩罚不同意他的人。

他死后，他的王朝很快就了。

即便如此，秦始皇永远改变了中国历史。

“China”这个名字甚至可能来自“案”这个词。
