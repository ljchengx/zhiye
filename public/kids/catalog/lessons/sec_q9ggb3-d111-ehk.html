Elephants: The Gentle Giants

Elephants are the largest animals on land.

They live in Africa and Asia.

In Thailand, the elephant is the national animal, and people celebrate Thai Elephant Day on 13 March every year.

Elephants look very different from other animals.

They are huge.

They have large ears and long trunks.

An elephant's trunk is very useful.

It can pick up food, carry heavy things, and even take a bath!

Elephants also have thick, grey skin and strong legs.
