Elephants: The Gentle Giants Elephants are the largest animals on land.

大象(学名:Gentle giants elephants)是陆地上最大的动物.

They live in Africa and Asia.

他们生活在非洲和亚洲。

In Thailand, the elephant is the national animal, and people celebrate Thai Elephant Day on 13 March every year.

在泰国,大象是国家动物,人们每年3月13日都会庆祝泰国大象节.

Elephants look very different from other animals.

大象的外观与其他动物非常不同.

They are huge.

它们是巨大的。

They have large ears and long trunks.

它们的耳朵很大,树干很长.

An elephant's trunk is very useful.

大象的树干非常有用.

It can pick up food, carry heavy things, and even take a bath!

它能捡起食物,搬运重物,甚至洗个澡!

Elephants also have thick, grey skin and strong legs.

大象也有厚,灰的皮肤和强壮的腿部.

Elephants are very clever.

大象很聪明

They can remember things for many years.

他们可以记住很多年的事情。

They can remember their friends, and they can remember where to find A better world for elephants.
