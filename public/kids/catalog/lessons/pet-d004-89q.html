World’s First 20MW Offshore Wind Turbine Installed（Jan 13）

World'sFirst20MW Offshore e Wind Turbine Installed (Jan 13) On January 13, the world's first 20-megawatt offshore wind turbine finishedinstallation in Fujian sea areas.

This marks a major breakthrough 中国三峡 20m20W% in China's large-capacity offshore wind power technology.

The unit stands more than 30 kilometres offshorewith water depthover40 metres.

After grid connection, it can generate over 80 million kilowatt-hours of electricity every year.

This power can satisfy the annual demand of about 44,000 families.

Compared with smaller turbines, the new model cutsconstruction costs andreducescarbon emissions.

Experts point out that large offshore wind equipment will speed up China's energy transition and support the development of clean renewable energy.
