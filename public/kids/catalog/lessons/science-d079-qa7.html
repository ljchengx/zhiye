Why Do Dolphins Love to Jump?

Why Do Dolphins Love to Jump?

海豚为什么喜欢跳跃？

Have you ever seen a dolphin leap high out of the water?

你见过海豚高高跃出水面的样子吗？

It looks like so much fun!

看起来真开心！

But why do they do it?

但它们为什么要这样做呢？

There are several reasons.

有好几个原因。

When dolphins swim fast, jumping out of the water

海豚快速游泳时，跃出水面反而能节省能量。

saves energy.

空气的阻力比水小得多。

Air has much less resistance than water.

通过跳跃，海豚可以用更少的力气游得更快。

By leaping, dolphins can swim faster with less effort.

海豚是非常喜欢社交的动物。

Dolphins are very social animals.

有时候，它们跳跃是为了给同伴传递信号。

Sometimes, they jump to send signals to their friends.

一连串的跳跃可能是在说“出发啦！

A series of leaps can mean "Let's go!

”或者“该回家啦！

or "Time to head home!" It is like a message that other dolphins can see from far away.

”这是一种远距离就能看到的信号。

Dolphins do not have hands to scratch themselves.

海豚没有手去挠痒痒。

One clever way they deal with parasites is by jumping and spinning in the air.

它们对付寄生虫的一个巧妙方法，就是跳出水面并在空中旋转。

The fast spin flings the parasites off their skin.

快速旋转能把寄生虫从皮肤上甩掉。

Some dolphins can spin up to 6 times per second!

有些海豚每秒能转6圈！

Dolphins are mammals, not fish.

海豚是哺乳动物，不是鱼。

They need to come to the surface to breathe.

它们需要浮出水面呼吸。

Sometimes, they use a jump to take a quick breath while traveling at high speeds.

有时候，它们会在高速游动时用一跃而起来快速换气。

Dolphins are playful and curious.

海豚爱玩又好奇。

Sometimes, they seem to jump just because it feels good.

有时候，它们跳跃好像就是因为一一开心！

Like children playing, they enjoy flying through the air and splashing back down.

就像爱玩的孩子一样，它们享受在空中飞翔然后扑通落水的感觉。
