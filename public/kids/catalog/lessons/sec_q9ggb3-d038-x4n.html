Li Bai: The Romantic Poet of China

Read and Learn 疑是地上霜 床前明月光 Li Bai:The Romantic Poet of China Li Bai is one of the greatest poets in Chinese history.

读与学 ⁇ 李白:中国浪漫诗人 李白是中国历史上最伟大的诗人之一.

He lived during the Tang Dynasty, about 1,300 years ago.

唐朝时期居住,约前1300年.

People call him the "Poet Immortal" because his poems are so beautiful and full of imagination.

人们称他为"诗不朽",因为他的诗歌如此美丽,充满想象力.

Even today, almost everyChinese child can recite some of his famous lines.

即使在今天,几乎每个中国孩子都可以背诵他的一些著名台词.

He wrote over 1,0o0 poems in his lifetime.

他一生写了超过10o0首诗.

Li Bai's poems are known for their love of nature, freedom, and friendship.

李白的诗歌以热爱自然,自由,友谊著称.

One of his most famous poems is "Thoughts on a Quiet Night," which begins: "Before my bed, the moonlight is bright.

他最著名的一首诗是"静夜的思念",开头是:"在我床前,月光明亮.

I think it is frost upon the ground." This short poem about homesickness is loved by Chinese people all over the world.

我认为是地面上的霜",这首关于思乡的短诗受到全世界中国人民的喜爱.

Another famous poem, "Drinking Alone Under the Moon," shows his romantic and free spirit.

另一首著名的诗"月下独饮"展现了他浪漫自由的精神.

Li Bai lived an adventurous life.

李白过着冒险的生活.

He traveled all over China, visiting mountains, rivers, and famous places.

他游历中国各地,游历山河,名胜胜地.

He loved wine and often wrote poems while drinking.

他喜爱葡萄酒,经常在喝酒时写诗.

It is said that he could write a beautiful poem in just a few minutes.

据说他在几分钟内就能写出一首美丽的诗.

He even met the emperor and became a palace poet for a time, but he preferred a free life of travel and writing.

他甚至遇见了皇帝,并成为了一段时间的宫廷诗人,但他更喜欢旅行和写作的自由生活.

Li Bai's poems teach us to see the beauty in the world and follow our hearts.

李白的诗歌教我们看清天下美人,跟着我们的心.

His words have been translated into many languages and are read by people everywhere.

他的言语被翻译成多种语言,各地的人都会阅读.

He is not just a Chinese treasure — he belongs to the whole world.

他不仅仅是中国的宝藏——他属于整个世界。

As long as people read poetry, Li Bai's spirit will live on.

只要人们读诗歌,李白的灵性就会继续活下去.
