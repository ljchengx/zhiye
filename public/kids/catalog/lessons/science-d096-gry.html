Why Do Our Teeth Fall Out?

Why D Do Our?

我们为什么会换牙？

TeethFall Out?

六岁左右，你的牙齿会开始松动、脱落。

When you are about six years old, your teeth start to get loose and fall out.

别担心一一这是好事！

Do not worry — this is a good thing!

说明你正在长大。

It means you are growing up.

你小时候的牙叫乳牙。

The teeth you have as a baby are called baby teeth.

它们很小，因为你的下巴也很小。

They are small because your jaw is small.

随着你长大，下巴变大了，就需要更大、更坚固的牙齿。

As you grow, your jaw gets bigger, and you need bigger, stronger teeth.

在乳牙下面，新的恒牙早就等好了。

Under your baby teeth, new adult teeth are waiting.

恒牙往上长，会顶着乳牙的牙根，乳牙就会慢慢松动、摇晃，最后掉下来，然后新牙就接替了它的位置！

As they grow up, they push on the roots of the baby teeth.

每天认真刷牙，好好爱护你的新牙，因为它们要陪你一辈子！

The baby tooth gets loose, wiggles, and finally falls out.

Then the new tooth takes its place!

Take good care of your new teeth by brushing every day.

They have to last your whole life!
