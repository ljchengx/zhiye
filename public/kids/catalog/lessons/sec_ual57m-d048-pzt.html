A Thank-You Card

A Thank-You Card

一张感谢卡

Tomorrow is Teachers' Day.

明天是教师节。

I want to make a thank-you card for Miss Wang.

我想为王老师制作一张感谢卡。

First, I fold a piece of red paper in half.

首先，我把一张红纸对折。

Then I draw a big heart on it.

接着，我在纸上画一颗大大的爱心。

Inside, I write: "Thank you for teaching us.

我在卡片里面写下：“谢谢您教导我们。

We love you!" When Miss Wang gets the card, she gives me a warm hug.

我们爱您！

当王老师收到卡片时，她给了我一个温暖的拥抱。
