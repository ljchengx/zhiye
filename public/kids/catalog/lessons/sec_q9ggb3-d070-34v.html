Butterflies Life Cycle

Butterflies Life Cycle Page 70 B English Text Butterflies are beautiful flying insects with colorful wings.

蝴蝶蜕变蝴蝶是拥有斑斓翅膀的美丽飞虫。

They go through four totally different stages in their whole life, and this amazing change is called a life cycle.

它们的一生会经历四个截然不同的阶段，这场奇妙的变化就叫做生命周期。

First, an adult butterfly lays tiny eggs on the back of green leaves.

首先，成蝴蝶会在绿叶背面产下细小的虫卵。

After a few days, small caterpillars hatch out from the eggs.

几天之后，毛毛虫就从卵中孵化出来。

Caterpillars eat leaves and continue to grow bigger and stronger.

毛毛虫会不停地啃食树叶，慢慢长大长壮。

When they grow up enough, the caterpillars will stop eating and wrap themselves up to become chrysalises.

当发育成熟后，毛毛虫便会停止进食，把自己包裹起来变成。

Inside the chrysalis, great changes are taking place.

的内部正在发生巨大的蜕变。

Finally, a lovely butterfly breaks out and starts its new life.

最终，一只漂亮的蝴蝶破茧而出，开启全新的生命旅程。
