Shadows change because the sun moves

across the sky.

In the morning, the sun is low.

Your shadow is long and stretches far.

At noon, the sun is high above you.

Your shadow becomes short, right under your feet.

In the afternoon, the sun goes down again.

Your shadow grows long once more.

The sun also changes shadows during different seasons.

In winter, the sun is lower, so shadows are longer.

In summer, the sun is higher, so shadows are shorter.

Shadows move and change all the time.

They are like quiet friends that follow you around!

The next time you see your shadow, watch it.

See how it changes during the day!
