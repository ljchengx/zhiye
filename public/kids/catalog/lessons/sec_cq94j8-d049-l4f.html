Lesson 79 Carol's shopping list

Carol's shopping list Lesson 79

卡罗尔的购物单

TOM What are you doing, Carol?

汤姆,你在做什么,卡罗尔?

CAROL I'm making a shopping list, Tom.

我做了个购物清单 汤姆

TOM What do we need?

汤姆,我们需要什么?

CAROL We need a lot of things this week.

CAROL,我们这周需要很多东西

3 I must go to the grocer's.

3,我必须去杂货店。

SUGAR COFFEE TEA CAROL We haven't got much tea or coffee, and we haven't got any sugar or jam.

苏格丽咖啡 茶卡罗尔 我们没有太多的茶或咖啡 我们没有糖或果酱

TOM What about vegetables?

汤姆,蔬菜呢?

CAROL I must go to the greengrocer's.

CAROL,我必须去绿葡萄园。

We haven't got many tomatoes, but we've got a lot of potatoes.

番茄品种不多,但马铃薯品种多.

5 MEAT CAROL I must go to the butcher's, too.

5米卡罗,我必须去 屠夫家,太。

We need some meat.

我们需要一些肉。

We haven't got any meat at all.

我们一点肉都没有

TOM Have we got any beer and wine?

汤姆,我们有啤酒和酒吗?

CAROL No, we haven't.

不,我们没有。

And I'm not going to get any!

我不会得到任何!

TOM I hope that you've got some money.

汤姆,我希望你有钱。

CAROL I haven't got much.

CAROL,我没有太多。

TOM Well, I haven't got much either!

汤姆,我也没什么!
