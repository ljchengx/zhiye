Our Closest Star Light Energy Life

The Sun is a huge, super hot, and glowing star in space.

太阳是太空中一颗巨大、超高温、持续发光的恒星。

It sits at the center of our solar system and controls the movement of all surrounding planets.

它位于太阳系正中心，控制着周围所有行星的运动。

Many young learners think that the Sun is a planet, but astronomers say it is a real star.

很多小朋友会误以为太阳是行星，但天文学家告诉我们：太阳是真正的恒星。

Compared to other stars, the Sun is the closest one to Earth.

在宇宙所有恒星中，太阳离地球最近。

That is why it looks much bigger and brighter than other stars in the night sky.

所以它看起来比夜空中任何星星都更大、更亮。

The Sun produces endless light and heat every second.

太阳每一秒都在产生源源不断的光和热。

This energy travels across space and reaches our Earth.

这些能量穿越太空到达地球。

It warms the land, lights up the day, helps plants grow, and supports all life.

温暖大地、照亮白昼、帮助植物生长，支撑所有生命。

Without the Sun, our planet would become a cold, dark, and lifeless world.

如果没有太阳，地球将变成一个寒冷、黑暗、毫无生机的星球。
