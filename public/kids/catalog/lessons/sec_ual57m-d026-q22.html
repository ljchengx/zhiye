Breakfast Time

早餐时间

It is seven o'clock in the morning.

现在是早上七点。

My family is having breakfast.

我们一家人正在吃早餐。

Dad is drinking milk and eating bread.

爸爸在喝牛奶、吃面包。

Mom is cooking eggs in the kitchen.

妈妈在厨房里煎鸡蛋。

My little brother is eating porridge with a spoon.

我的小弟弟在用勺子喝粥。

I am having noodles.

我在吃面条，面条又热又香。

They are hot and nice.

早餐给我们一天的能量。

Breakfast gives us energy for the day.

我们早上都吃得很饱。

We all eat well in the morning.
