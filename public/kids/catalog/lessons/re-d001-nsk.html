What Is Qixi

Read and Learn What Is Qixi?

Do you know that China has a special day called Qixi?

It falls on the 7th day of the 7th month of the lunar calendar.

It is one of the oldest festivals in China - over 2,000 years old!

In the past, Qixi was a day for girls to show their skills.

They would sew, weave, and make beautiful crafts.

They prayed for clever hands and a bright mind.

That is why it was also called Qiqiao Jie - the Festival of Praying for Skills.

People also celebrated the harvest.

The 7th month of the lunar calendar is when many crops are ready to be picked.

Families would gather and enjoy the fruits of their hard work.

Today, Qixi has become a day to show love to family and friends.

People give small gifts, write cards, or just spend time with the people they care about.

Qixi reminds us that love, skill, and hard work are all worth celebrating.
