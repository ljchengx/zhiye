How Does a Performer Change Faces in the Blink of an Eye?

Read and Learn How Does a Performer Change Faces in the Blink of an Eye?

Have you ever seen a performer change his face in less than a second?

That is Sichuan Opera's famous trick called "Bian Lian," or face changing.

It is a special skill from traditional Chinese theater in Sichuan Province.

In 2006, it was listed as a national intangible cultural heritage of China.

The most amazing part is that the actor wears colorful masks and swaps them instantly.

No one can see how he does it!

He hides many thin masks under his costume.

With quick flicks of his fan, cloak, or even his hand, he changes from red to green, from angry to happy.

Each color and pattern means a different feeling or character.

There is a fun legend behind this skill.

Long ago, people painted their faces to scare away wildanimals.

Later, actorsusedmaskstoshow sudden changes in emotion.

Some say the secret of Bian Lian is passed down only from master to student, and 1 performersmust practice for years.

Even today, the trick remains a mystery to most audiences.

Bian Lian is not just a showit is a treasure of Chinese culture.

Young people all over the worldlovewatchingitonvideosandat festivals.

Itshowsthatartcanbebothancient and exciting.
