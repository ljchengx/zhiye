Fish Can Breathe Underwater

English Text

鱼儿终生生活在水中，它们没有人类这样的肺。

Fish live underwater all their lives.

鱼类依靠鱼鳃呼吸。

They do not have lungs like us.

水流经过鱼鳃时，鱼儿就能吸收水中的氧气。

Fish use gills to breathe.

鱼离开水无法长时间存活，只有在水里才能顺畅呼吸。

When water passes through their gills, they take oxygen from the water.

呼吸器官：鱼鳃（人类用肺）原理：从水流中获取氧气特点：离不开水

Fish cannot stay out of water for long.

They can only breathe freely in water.
