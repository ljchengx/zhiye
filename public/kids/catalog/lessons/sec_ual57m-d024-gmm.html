My Favorite Teacher

My Favorite Teacher My favorite teacher is Miss Wang.

我最喜欢的老师我最喜欢的老师是王老师。

She teaches us English.

她教我们英语。

She is tall and kind.

她个子高高的，很和蔼。

Her classes are very interesting.

她的课非常有趣。

She often sings English songs with us.

她经常和我们一起唱英文歌。

When I can't read a word, she helps me.

当我不会读一个单词时，她会帮助我。

Her smile is Warm like the sun.

她的笑容像太阳一样温暖。

We all love her.

我们都爱她。

I want to be a teacher like her one day.

我希望有一天能成为像她一样的老师。
