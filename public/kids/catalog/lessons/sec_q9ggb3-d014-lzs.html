Sun Wukong – The Monkey King

Read and Learn Wukong Sun King TheMonkey Sun Wukong, also called the Monkey King, comes from a famous Chinese book called Journey to the West.

magical /'mad31kl/ 神奇的，魔法的 孙悟空，也叫美猴王，出自中国著名的小说《西游记》。

He was born from a magical stone on a mountain.

他是由山上的一块仙石 孕育而生的。

He is brave, clever, and sometimes very naughty.

他勇敢、聪明，有时也很调皮。

Many Chinese children grow up hearing his stories.

The Monkey King has amazing powers.

He can turn into 72 different forms, such as a bird, a tree, or even a tiny bug.

他会七十二变，可以变成鸟、树，甚至一只小虫子。

He can jump very far and ride on clouds.

他 能跳得很远，还会腾云驾雾。

weapon /'wepan/ 武器 他最著名的武器是金箍棒。

At first, it was used to measure the depth of the sea, butSun Wukong took it and made it his own.

最初，它是用来测量海水深度的，但孙悟空把它拿来当作了自己的武器。

With this staff, he protects his master Tang Monk during their journey to the west.
