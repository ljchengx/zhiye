Fruit Salad

Fruit Salad

水果沙拉

Todaywemake afruit salad in our cookingclass.

今天我们在烹饪课上做水果沙拉。

First, wewash the apples, bananas and grapes.

首先，我们洗苹果、香蕉和葡萄；然后，给香蕉和橙子剥皮；接着，把所有水果切成小块；之后，把它们放进一个大碗里；最后，加一些酸奶搅拌均匀。

Then, we peel the bananas and oranges.

沙拉看起来五彩缤纷，尝起来甜甜的。

Next, we cut all the fruit into small pieces.

我们和老师一起分享，每个人都很喜欢！

After that, weput them in a bigbowl.

Finally, we add some yogurt and mix everything together.

The salad looks colourful and tastes sweet.

Weshareitwithourteacher.

Everyonelovesit!
