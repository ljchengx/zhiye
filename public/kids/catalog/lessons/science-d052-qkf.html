Tree Rings: Nature's Time Machine

in Life

中文翻译 年轮：大自然的时光机你见过树桩上面一圈一圈的纹路吗？

Tree Rings:

那些就叫“年轮”。

Nature's Time Machine

它们就像大树写了很多年的秘密日记！

Have you ever seen a tree stump with circles on top?

每过一年，大树就长出一圈新的年轮。

Those circles are called tree rings.

所以数一数年轮，就能知道这棵树多少岁了—一圈年轮=一年！

They are like a secret diary that the treekeeps for many years!

但年轮告诉我们的不止是年龄。

Every year, a tree grows a new ring.

仔细看，有些年轮宽，有些年轮窄。

So if you count the rings, you can tell how old the tree is.

宽的年轮说明那一年雨水多、阳光好，大树过得很滋润；窄的年轮说明那一年可能很干旱或者很寒冷。

One ring one year!

年轮还能告诉我们很久以前发生的故事。

How Tree Rings Form 1 Spring ②Summer?

如果年轮上有一道疤痕，可能那一年发生了火灾。

The tree starts to grow.

如果年轮长得歪歪扭扭，可能那一年有大风暴把树吹得摇摇晃晃。

Warm weather helps it grow.

所以下次你看到树桩的时候，低头看看它的年轮一那棵树正在一圈一圈地给你讲它一生的故事呢！

Fall: ④Winter

But tree rings tell us more than just age.

When you look closely, some rings are wide, and some are narrow.

Wide rings mean the tree had a good year with lots of rain and sunshine.

Narrow rings mean the year was dry or cold.

Growth slows down.

The tree rests.

Wide Rings vs.

Narrow Rings

Widerings: Narrowrings

The rings can also tell us stories from long ago.

If a tree has a scar on its rings, maybe a fire happened that year.

If the rings are crooked, maybe a big storm pushed the tree around.

So next time you see a tree stump, take a look at its rings.

That tree is telling you its whole life story — one ring at a time!
