National Day – A Celebration of Our Great Country

National Day, a Celebration of our great Country.

国庆节，是我们伟大祖国的庆典。

October 1st is a very special day for every Chinese person.

10月1日对每一个中国人来说都是一个非常特殊的日子。

It is China's national day.

今天是中国的国庆节。

On this day in 1949, the People's Republic of China was founded.

1949年的这一天，中华人民共和国成立了。

Every year we celebrate this day with joy and pride.

每年我们都满怀喜悦和自豪地庆祝这一天。

When we see the 5 -star red flag flying in the sky we feel proud.

当我们看到五星红旗在天空中飘扬时，我们感到自豪。

The flag is bright red, like a flame that lights up the land.

这面旗帜鲜红如火，如同照亮大地的火焰。

The five stars on the flag shine together showing the unity of the Chinese people.

国旗上的五颗星星共同闪耀，象征着中国人民的团结。

China is a Country with a long history and a rich culture.

中国是一个历史悠久、文化丰富的国家。

It has beautiful mountains, long rivers, and many great cities.

它有美丽的山脉、悠长的河流和许多大城市。

The Chinese people are hardworking, brave, and kind.

中国人民勤劳、勇敢、善良。

They love their Country, and are always ready to help each other.

他们热爱自己的国家，总是乐于互相帮助。

National Day reminds us of the hard work and sacrifice of those who came before us.

国庆节让我们想起那些前辈们的辛勤工作和牺牲。

They built our Country and gave us a better life.

他们建设了我们的国家，并为我们带来了更美好的生活。

It also reminds us to be grateful and to work hard for a brighter future.

它还提醒我们要心怀感激，并为更美好的未来而努力。

Many people celebrate by traveling, spending time with family, or watching the flag raising ceremony.

许多人通过旅行、与家人共度时光或观看升旗仪式来庆祝。

But no matter how we celebrate, we all share one thing love for our Country.

但无论我们如何庆祝，我们都共同拥有一个特质——对祖国的热爱。

Happy National Day!

国庆节快乐！

May our Country always be strong and prosperous.

愿我们的国家永远强大繁荣。
