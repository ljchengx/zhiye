Chinese Porcelain — A Treasure of the World

Chinese Porcelain

A Treasure of the World Chinese porcelain is one of the greatest inventions in human history.

People in China began making pottery thousands of years ago, and by the Tang and Song dynasties, Chinese porcelain had become famous all over the world.

It was not only beautiful but also very strong, and it could hold water and heatwell.

The city of Jingdezhen, in Jiangxi Province, is called the‘Porcelain Capital’of China.

For over a thousand years, skilled workers there made the finest porcelain in the world.

One of the most famous types is blue-and-white porcelain.

It has beautiful blue patterns painted on a white background, often showing flowers, rivers, or dragons.

Chinese porcelain traveled to other countries along the Silk Road, both by land and by sea.

People in Europe and theMiddle East loved it very much.

They called it ‘china' because it came from China.

Even today, in English, the word ‘china’ means both the country and fine porcelain.

Chinese porcelain is not just a useful object — it is a piece of art and history.

It shows the wisdom and creativity of the Chinese people, and it is still loved by people all around the world.

中文翻译：

中国陶瓷是人类历史上最伟大的发明之一。

中国人几千年前就开始制作陶器，到了唐宋时期已经闻名世界，不仅美丽而且坚固，能装水也能保温。

景德镇被称为中国瓷都，一千多年来制作出世界上最精美的瓷器，最著名的是青花瓷，白色底上有漂亮的蓝色花纹。

中国陶瓷通过丝绸之路传到其他国家，欧洲和中东的人们非常喜爱，把它叫做china。

中国陶瓷不仅是物品，更是艺术和历史，展示了中国人民的智慧和创造力。
