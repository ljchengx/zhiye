The Chinese Knot: Tying Luck and Beauty Together

Read and Learn 衣首 The Chinese Knot: 芙吉 Tying Luck and Beauty Together The Chinese knot is a beautiful handmade decoration made from a single piece of red cord.

阅读与学习 QQ 中文小结:QQ 捆绑运气与美人合在一起 中文结是一个用单片红线制作的漂亮的手工装饰.

It has a history of over 1,000 years.

其历史年代为1000多年.

In ancient times, people used knots to record events before writing was invented.

在古代,人们在写作被发明之前就用结来记录事件.

Later, knots became decorations and symbols of good luck.

后来,结成为装饰和好运的象征.

Today, the Chinese knot is one of the most loved traditional crafts in China.

如今,中国结是中国最受喜爱的传统工艺品之一.

A Chinese knot is always made from one continuous cord, with no beginning and no end.

中国的结总是用一条连续的绳索来制成的,没有起点,也没有终点.

This represents the idea of "never-ending' endless happiness, endless love, and endless good fortune.

这代表了"永不结束"无尽的幸福,无尽的爱,无尽的好运气"的理念.

The knot is usually red, because red means happiness and good luck in Chinese culture.

结通常为红色,因为红色在中国文化中意味着幸福和好运.

Sometimes people add small bells, jade, or coins to the knot to make it even more special.

有时人们会在结上加入小铃,玉,或硬币,使其更加特别.

There are many different types of Chinese knots.

中国结有很多不同的种类.

The most common one is the "double coin knot," which looks like two old Chinese coins and stands for wealth.

最常见的是"双钱币结",它看起来像两枚中国老钱币,代表着财富.

There is also the "button knot," the "cross knot," and the"Pan Chang knot."Each pattern has its own meaning and beauty.

还有"扣结","横结","潘昌结",每一种图案都有其本意和美感.

Making a Chinese knot takes patience and skill, but the result is always wonderful.

做一个中国结需要耐心和技巧,但结果总是很好.

During Chinese NewYear and other festivals, you can see Chinese knots hanging in homes, shops, and streets.

在中国新年等节日期间,可以看到中国结在家中,商店和街道上悬挂.

People also give Chinese knots as gifts to family and friends.

人们也给中国结作为礼物送给亲友.

A Chinese knot is more than a decoration —- it carries wishes for health, happiness, and a bright future.

中国结不仅仅是装饰——它带有对健康,幸福,以及光明未来的愿望.
