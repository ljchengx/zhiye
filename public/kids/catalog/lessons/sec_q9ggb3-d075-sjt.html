Doqs Good Listeners

English Text

狗狗是全球最受欢迎的宠物之一。

Dogs are one of the most popular pets all over the world.

它们的耳朵大而灵敏，听觉远胜于人类。

They have big and sensitive ears,so their sense of hearing is much better than humans.

狗狗能够捕捉到细微的声响，以及来自远方的动静。

They can catch soft sounds and noises from far away.

除了出色的听力，狗狗还以忠诚、友善、聪慧著称。

Besides good hearing, dogs are famous for being loyal, friendly and smart.

它们会和主人建立深厚的感情。

They build deep friendship with their owners.

狗狗能快速学会简单的指令和技能。

Dogs are quick to learn simple orders and skills.

有的狗狗帮忙看家，经过专业训练的工作犬，还可以引导盲人，救助遇险的人，它们当之无愧是人类最好的伙伴。

Some dogs help people look after homes, and trained working dogs can even guide blind people or rescue people in danger.

1.特点：听觉灵敏、忠诚温顺、智商高 填空：Dogs have good救援等工作 英译中：loyal

They are truly man's best friends.
