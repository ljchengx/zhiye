How Does a Thermometer Work?

体温计是怎么工作的？

Thermometer Work?

发烧的时候，体温计能告诉你身体有多热。

When you have a fever, a thermometer tells you how hot your body is.

但这个小工具是怎么知道你的温度的呢？

But how does this little tool know your temperature?

老式体温计里有一根细细的玻璃管，管里装着银色的液体，叫水银。

An old-style thermometer has a tiny

把它放在腋下时，你身体的热量会把液体热。

glass tube with silver liquid inside.

巧妙的地方来了：液体受热后会膨胀变大，顺着管子往上升。

This liquid is called mercury.

你越烧，它爬得越高！

When you put it under your arm, your body heat warms the liquid.

看液柱顶端对应的数字，就是你的体温。

Here is the clever part: when the liquid gets warm, it grows bigger and rises up the tube.

现在很多家庭用电子体温计，它里面是一个小小的电子传感器，测好了还会嘀嘀提醒你。

The hotter you are, the higher it climbs!

科学帮助我们照顾好自己的健康！

You read the number next to the top of the liquid, and that is your temperature.

Today many families use digital thermometers.

They use a tiny electronic sensor instead, and beep when they are done.

Science helps us take care of our health!

Mercury rises when it gets warm.
