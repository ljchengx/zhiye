What Is the Dragon Boat Festival

Read and Learn* What Isthe Dragon Boat Festival?

阅读和学习 * 什么是龙舟节?

The Dragon Boat Festival is a special holiday in China.

龙舟节是中国的一个特殊节日.

It is on the fifth day of the fifth month of the lunar year.

是月五日正月五日时.

People celebrate it to remember a great poet named Qu Yuan.

众人庆贺,以纪念一位名叫邱元的伟大诗人.

He lived a very long time ago.

他活了很久了,他活了很久了。

Long ago, Qu Yuan loved his country very much.

久之,奎元甚爱其国.

He was sad when his country was in trouble.

国难时他感到悲伤.

He jumped into the river and died.

他跳进河里死了.

People were very sad.

人们非常伤心。

They rowed boats to find him and threw rice into the water tofeed thefish.

他们划船去找他,然后把大米扔进水里喂鱼。

Today, we have dragon boat races.

今天,我们有龙船比赛。

The boats look like dragons and are very long.

船形似龙,长甚长.

Teams of people row the boats very fast.

一群人迅速划船

We also eat zongzi, which are rice dumplings wrapped in bamboo leaves.

我们也吃宗子,是用竹叶包裹的米饺子.

They are very yummy.

他们非常好吃。

People also hang mugwort on their doors and tie colorful silk threads on their arms.

人们还在门上挂着毛巾,在手臂上绑着彩色丝线.

These are for good luck and health.

这些都是为了好运和健康。

The Dragon Boat Festival is a time to remember history and have fun with family.

龙舟节纪念历史 与家人共乐.
