Mom's Kitchen

妈妈的厨房

My mom's kitchen is small but warm.

妈妈的厨房虽小，却很温馨。

There is a big fridge near the door.

门旁边有一台大冰箱，橱柜里有许多盘子和碗。

There are many plates and bowls in the cupboard.

看！

Look!

妈妈正在做晚饭。

Mom is making dinner now.

她在切西红柿、洗土豆。

She is cutting tomatoes and washing potatoes.

炉子上的汤闻起来真香。

The soup on the stove smells good.

我经常在厨房给妈妈帮忙，我摆桌子、洗蔬菜。

I often help Mom in the kitchen.

一起做饭真有趣。

I set the table and wash the vegetables.

Cooking together is fun.
