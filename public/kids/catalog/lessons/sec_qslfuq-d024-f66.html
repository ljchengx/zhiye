Du Fu Thatched Cottage

Du Fu Thatched Cottage The Du Fu Thatched Cottage is in Chengdu,

Sichuan.

杜甫草堂位于四川成都，是

It was the old home of Du Fu, a great

poet of the Tang dynasty.

唐代大诗人杜甫的旧居。

The simple house has a thatched roof.

这座简朴的房子有着茅草屋顶，经过

It has

been rebuilt to show how the poet once lived.

复原重建，展现诗人当年的生活。

草堂四周是一处幽静的园

Around the cottage there is a quiet garden, with

林，有翠竹、池塘和古树。

green bamboo, ponds and old trees.

杜甫在这里写下了许多名诗，草

Du Fu wrote many famous poems here.

堂是诗歌爱好者喜爱的好地方。

The cottage is a lovely place for people who love poetry.

poet thatched bamboo pond poem
