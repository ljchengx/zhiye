What Is the Spring Festival?

Read and Learn What Is the Spring Festival?

读习春节是什么?

The Spring Festival is the most important holiday in China.

春节是中国最重要的节日.

It is also called Chinese New Year.

也称"中国新年".

It usually comes in January or February.

通常在1月或2月才出现.

People all over China celebrate it with joy.

中国各地人民欢喜地庆祝.

Before the festival, families do a big cleaning.

节前,家人做大清扫.

They 新春大吉 clean their homes to sweep away bad luck.

他们清理他们的家 扫走坏运气。

Then they put up red couplets on the doors.

然后在门上贴上红色的对子.

Red is a lucky color in China.

红色是中国的一种幸运颜色.

On New Year's Eve, families get together for a big dinner.

除夕,诸家聚聚,共宴大宴.

We eat dumplings, fish, and many other tasty foods.

我们吃饺子,鱼, 和许多其他美味的食物。

Dumplings look like old Chinese money, so they mean good luck.

扁桃花看起来像中国老钱,所以意思是好运.

After dinner, children get red envelopes with money inside.

晚餐后,孩子们会得到红色的信封,里面有钱.

There is an old story about a monster called Nian.

有个老故事说有个叫尼安的怪物

Nian came out to hurt people every New Year.

尼安每到新年就出来伤害人.

People found that Nian was afraid of the color red and loud noises.

人们发现,尼安害怕颜色的红色和响亮的噪音.

So they set off firecrackers and used red things to scare it away.

于是他们点燃了鞭炮,并用红色的东西来吓走它.

Now we still do these things to celebrate.

现在,我们仍然做这些事情来庆祝。
