Shadow Puppetry

Shadow Puppetry: Stories Behind a Screen

非物质文化遗产

Readand Learn

皮影戏是一种古老的中国讲故事艺术，已有两千多年的历史。

Shadow puppetry is an ancient Chinese art of storytelling.

传说汉武帝失去了心爱的妃子，非常思念她。

It began over 2,000 years ago.

一位术士用灯光让她的影子在幕布上移动，皮影戏就这样诞生了。

Legend says that Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty lost his favorite wife.

制作皮影时，艺人会选择兽皮，比如牛皮或羊皮。

He missed her so much that a magician made her shadow move on a screen using light.

他们把皮削薄，雕刻出精美的花纹，再涂上鲜艳的颜色，最后在四肢装上小棍。

This is how shadow puppetry was born.

一个好皮影可能要花几周才能完成！

To make a puppet, artists choose animal skin, like cow or sheep skin.

表演时，艺人们站在白色幕布后面，把皮影贴近幕布，操纵小棍。

They cut it into thin pieces, then carve beautiful patterns.

明亮的灯光从后面照过来，观众就能看到彩色的影子。

After that, they paint it with bright colors.

艺人们还会为角色唱歌、说话，有时甚至演奏音乐！

Finally, they attach sticks to the arms andlegs.

如今，皮影戏被列入联合国教科文组织非物质文化遗产名录。

A goodpuppet can take weeks to finish!

During a show, puppeteers stand behind a white clothscreen.

Theyhold the puppets close to the screen and move the sticks.

A bright lamp shines from behind, so the audience seescolorful shadows.

The puppeteers also sing and speak for the characters.

Sometimes they even play music!

Today, shadow puppetry is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.

Many schools and museums teachit to children.

Some artists use modern technology, like LED lights and videos, to make it more exciting.

This old art keeps shining in new ways.
