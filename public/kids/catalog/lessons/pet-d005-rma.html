XionganNewArea Speeds up

High-quality y Construction

On May 10, important research work was carried out in Xiongan New Area.

The new area is built to ease non-capital functions of Beijing and push balancedregional development.

After years of steady construction, infrastructure such as roads, underground pipe networks and public service buildings has taken shape.

The city sticks to eco-friendly planning, making green spaces an important part of daily living areas.

Planners point out that Xiongan will keep focusing on high-end industries and modern public services.

People look forward to the new city growing into a model of smart, low-carbon and livable urban development in northern China.
