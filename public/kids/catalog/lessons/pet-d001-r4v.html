The Mystery of Sanxingdui

The Sanxingdui site is in Guanghan, Sichuan Province.

三星堆遗址位于四川省广汉市。

It is about 3,000 to 5,000 years old.

它距今约3000—5000年。

Farmers discovered it in 1929, and archaeologists found many treasures in the 1980s.

1929年被农民发现，20世纪80年代考古学家出土了大量珍宝。

They found bronze masks, statues, gold ornaments, jade, and ivory.

出土了青铜面具、青铜立人像、金器、玉器和象牙等。

The bronze masks have huge eyes and unusual shapes.

青铜面具眼睛巨大、造型奇特。

There are no written records, so many questions remain.

由于没有文字记载，许多问题仍是谜。

The treasures are now in the Sanxingdui Museum.

这些珍宝如今陈列在三星堆博物馆。
