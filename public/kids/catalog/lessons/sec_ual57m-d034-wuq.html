Wind and Rain

风和雨

The sky is dark now.

天空现在阴沉沉的。

The wind is blowing strongly.

风刮得很大，树叶在空中飞舞。

The leaves are dancing in the air.

很快，雨来了。

Soon, the rain is coming.

外面下着大雨，窗户啪嗒作响。

It is raining heavily outside.

我的小妹妹有点害怕。

The windows are making sounds.

我拉着她的手说：“别担心，雨很快就会停的。

My little sister is a little afraid.

”雨后，空气清新，天空中出现了一道美丽的彩虹。

I hold her hand and say, "Don't worry.

The rain will stop soon." After the rain, the air is fresh.

A beautiful rainbow is in the sky.
