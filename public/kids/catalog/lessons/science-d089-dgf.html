Why Do Owls Turn Their Heads?

Why Do Owls

猫头鹰为什么能转动头？

Turn Their Heads?

Owls can turn their heads almost all the way around!

It looks like a magic trick.

How do they do it?

An owl's eyes are fixed in its head.

It cannot move them left or right like we can.

So to look around, the owl must turn its whole head.

Luckily, an owl has fourteen bones in its neck - twice as many as us!

These special bones let the head turn very far, up to 270 degrees.

That is three-quarters of a full circle!

This super neck helps the owl hunt at night.

It can sit very still and watch for a little mouse in every direction.

No wonder the owl is such a great night hunter!
