My Week.

On Monday, I wave hello and make a brand new start.

星期一，我挥手问好，开始新的一周。

On Sunday, I rest and get ready for a new week.

星期日，我休息，为新的一周做准备。

On Saturday, I stay with family out in the sun.

星期六，我和家人一起在阳光下。

On Friday, I play with friends and have some fun.

星期五，我和朋友们一起玩。

On Tuesday, I learn new things and do my part.

星期二，我学习新东西，做好自己的事。

On Wednesday, I help my friends and we all laugh and play.

星期三，我帮助朋友，我们一起笑、一起玩。

On Thursday, I draw a picture in a happy way.

星期四，我开心地画一幅画。
