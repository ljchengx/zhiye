At the Supermarket

At the Supermarket

在超市

On Saturday morning, Mom and I go to the supermarket.

星期六早上，妈妈和我去超市。

It is big and bright.

超市又大又明亮，有很多人在购物。

There are many people shopping there.

首先，我们推了一辆购物车。

First, we take a shopping cart.

然后我们来到水果区，苹果红红的，香蕉黄黄的。

Then we go to the fruit area.

妈妈买了一些蔬菜、牛奶和鸡蛋，我选了一盒果汁。

The apples are red and the bananas are yellow.

最后，我们排队在收银台结账。

Mom buys some vegetables, milk and eggs.

购物袋装得满满的，沉甸甸的。

I choose a box of juice.

At last, we wait in line and pay at the checkout.

The shopping bag is full and heavy.
