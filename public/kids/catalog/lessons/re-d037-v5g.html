Du Fu: The Poet Who Cared for the People

Read and Learn Du Fu: The Poet Who Cared for thePeople 堂草甫杜 Du Fu is one of the greatest poets of the Tang Dynasty, often called the "Sage of Poetry." He lived about 1,300 years ago, around the same time as Li Bai.

读书学杜甫:"爱民的诗人

But while Li Bai wrote about romance, nature, and the moon, Du Fu wrote about real life, the suffering of ordinary people, and his deep love for his country.

但是,虽然李白写了浪漫,自然,月亮,但杜甫却写了真实的生活,普通人的痛苦,以及他对祖国的深情.

The two poets were good friends.

两位诗人都是好友.

Du Fu lived during a time of war and chaos in China.

杜甫在中国战乱时期生活.

He saw people losing their homes, going hungry, and suffering in the cold.

他看见人们失去家园,挨饿,在寒冷中受苦.

His poems reflect this sadness and 下筝如有神杜甫 读书破罗卷 his wish for a better world.

他的诗反映了这种悲哀 和他对于更美好世界的愿望。

One of his most famous poems is "Spring View," which describes a city destroyed by war but with flowers still growing in the ruins.

他最著名的诗作之一是"春景",其中描述了一座被战争摧毁但废墟中仍然种植着鲜花的城市.

It shows both hope and heartbreak.

既显示希望又让人心碎.

Du Fu's poems are called "poetic history" because they record real events of his time.

杜甫的诗歌被称为"诗史",因为诗歌记录了他当时的真实事件.

He wrote about his family, his travels, his poverty, and his dreams.

他写了家事,旅行,贫穷,梦境.

Unlike Li Bai's carefree style, Du Fu's poems are deep, serious, and full of emotion.

与李白的无忧无虑风格不同,杜甫的诗歌深沉,严肃,充满情感.

Every word carries weight.

每个字都带有重量.

He spent his life thinking about how to make the world better, even when he himself was struggling.

他一生都在思考如何让世界变得更美好,即使他自己也在挣扎.

Today, Du Fu is loved by millions of people around the world.

如今,杜甫受到全世界数百万人的爱戴.

His cottage in Chengdu has become a famous museum.

其在成都的小屋成为著名的博物馆.

People visit to feel closer to this great poet who taught us to care about others, even in hard times.

人们来此是为了更接近这位伟大的诗人,他教我们关心他人,即使在困难时期.

Du Fu shows us that poetry can be a powerful way to speak for the voiceless and remember what truly matters.

杜甫向我们展示,诗歌可以成为为无声者说话的有力方式,记住真正重要的.
