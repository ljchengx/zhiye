The Chinese Dragon

The Chinese Dragon The Chinese dragon is a magical animal in Chinese stories.

It has a long body like a snake, horns like a deer, and claws like an eagle.

Its head looks like a horse, and its body is covered with shining scales.

Unlike the scary dragons in the West, the Chinese dragon is kind and wise.

In Chinese culture, the dragon stands for good luck, power, and happiness.

People say that dragons control rain and wind, so farmers pray to them for a good harvest.

The dragon is also a symbol of China.

Many Chinese people call themselves "descendants of the dragon."

Western dragons are usually big, fire-breathing monsters that guard treasure and fight heroes.

But Chinese dragons are peaceful.

They live in rivers, lakes, and seas, and they bring water to the land.

So Chinese people love and respect the dragon.

During festivals, especially the Spring Festival, people perform the dragon dance.
