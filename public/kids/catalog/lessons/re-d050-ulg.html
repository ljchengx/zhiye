Why Is Everyone Wearing a "Horse-Face" Skirt?

Why Is

标题：为什么大家都穿“马面裙”？

Everyone Wearing a"Horse-Face' Skirt?

核心单词

Have you ever seen a skirt with a flat panel in the front and pleats on the sides?

你见过前面有一块平整布料、两侧有裙福的裙子吗？

That is the Mamian skirt,also called the"horse-face skirt."It is a traditionalskirtwornbyHanChinesewomenin ancienttimes.

那就是马面裙。

Peoplestartedwearingitover800 years ago, during the Song and Liao dynasties.

这是中国古代汉族女性穿的传统裙子。

It became very popular in the Ming and Qing dynasties.

人们从800多年前的宋辽时期就开始穿它了。

The most special part of the Mamian skirt is its design.

它在明清时期非常波行。

It has two flat, smooth panels—one in the front and one in the back—called"horse faces."Between the panels, there are many pleats,which make the skirt wideandeasy towalkin.

马面裙最特别的地方是它的设计。

Thepleats alsocreate a beautifulwave-like shape when the wearer moves.

它有前后两块平整光滑的裙门，叫做“马面”。

OldMamianskirtswereoftendecoratedwithcolorful embroidery and patterns.

两块裙门之间有很多裙裙，这让裙子很宽大，走路也方便。

Here is a fun fact:the name"horse-face"does not mean the skirt looks like a horse!

当穿着者动起来时，福褐会形成美丽的波浪形状。

Instead,it comes from a type of ancient city gate called a "horse face wall."The flat panel looks like that part of the wall.

老式的马面裙常常绣有彩色花纹和图案。

In the past,women wore Mamian skirts for festivals, weddings,and important ceremonies.

这里有个有趣的知识：“马面”这个名字并不是说裙子看起来像马！

Some skirts were so long that they touched the ground, showing the wearer's grace and status.

它其实来自一种古代城墙的构造，叫“马画墙”。

Today,the Mamian skirt is making a big comeback.

过去，女性在节日、婚礼和重要仪式上穿马面裙。

Young people in China love to wear it with T-shirts ormodernjackets.

有些裙子特别长，拖到地上，显示出穿着者的优雅和身份。

Itmixes old andnewstyles in a cool way.

如今，马面裙正在强回归。

Many students wear it to school or on trips.

中国的年轻人喜欢用T恤或现代外套措配马面裙。

The skirt is not just clothing——-it is a bridge between history and today.

很多学生穿着它去上学或旅行。

So next time you see one,remember: you arelooking at 800years of Chinese culture!

马面裙不只是衣服一一它是连接历史和今天的桥梁。

所以下次你看到它时，请记住：你看到的是800年的中国文化！
