The Chinese Kite

Read and Learn The Chinese Lite The kite was invented in China more than 2,000 years ago.

It is one of the oldest flying objects made by humans.

According to an old story, Mozi, a famous Chinese philosopher, spent three years making a wooden bird that could fly in the sky.

That was the first kite in history.

In ancient times, kites were not just toys.

Soldiers used them to send messages or measure distances.

Some people even used kites to scare enemies in battle.

Later, kites became a popular game for children and adults.

People flew them during festivals and holidays.

Today, the city of Weifang in Shandong is called the "Kite Capital of the World." Every April, the Weifang International Kite Festival is held there.

People from all over the world come to fly their beautiful and creative kites.

In Chinese culture, kites mean good luck and happiness.

The Chinese word for kite is "fengzheng," which sounds like "wind and zheng' (a kind of music instrument).

Flying a kite is also a way to wish for health and a bright future.
