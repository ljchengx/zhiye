Lesson 69 The car race

The car race

汽车比赛

There is a car race near our town every year.

每年我们镇附近都会有一场赛车比赛

In 1995, there was a very big race.

1995年,赛事规模非常大.

2 There were hundreds of people there.

2 那里有数百人。

My wife and I were at the race.

我妻子和我参加了比赛。

Our friends Julie and Jack were there, too.

我们的朋友Julie和Jack也在场

You can see us in the crowd.

你可以在人群中看到我们。

We are standing on the left.

我们站在左边。

3 There were twenty cars in the race.

3,赛车共20辆.

There were English cars, French cars, German cars, Italian cars, American cars and Japanese cars.

当时有英国车,法国车,德国车,意大利车,美国车和日本车.

4 It was an exciting finish.

4,这是一个令人兴奋的结局。

The winner was Billy Stewart.

获胜者是比利·斯图尔特.

He was in car number fifteen.

他当时在十五号车厢

Five other cars were just behind him.

还有五辆车就在他后面

5 On the way home, Don't drive so quickly!

5,回家的路上,不要开这么快!

my wife said to me, You're not Billy Stewart!

我老婆跟我说 你不是比利・斯图尔特!

'Don't drive so quickly!

别开这么快!

You're not Billy Stewart!'

你不是比利·斯图尔特!"
