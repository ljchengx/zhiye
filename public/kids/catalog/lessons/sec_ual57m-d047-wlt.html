The Spring Festival

The Spring Festival

春节

The Spring Festival is the most important festival in China.

福春节是中国最重要的节日。

Families clean their houses and put up red couplets.

家家户户打扫房屋，贴上红色的春联。

On New Year's Eve, we have a big dinner.

除夕夜，我们吃一顿丰盛的年夜饭。

Children love it best because they can get red packets.

孩子们最喜欢这个节日，因为他们能收到红包。

At midnight, fireworks light up the sky.

午夜时分，烟花照亮了夜空。

Red is everywhere.

到处都是红色，它代表着幸福和好运。

It means happiness and good luck.
