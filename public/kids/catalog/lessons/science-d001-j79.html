The Secrets of Ice Cream Production

The Secrets of Ice Cream Production.

冰淇淋制作的秘密。

Ice cream is the most popular treat on hot summer days.

在炎热的夏日，冰淇淋是最受欢迎的甜品。

It is cold and smooth on the tongue when you lick it, and its sweet taste melts away instantly on your tongue.

当你舔它时，舌头会感到冰凉而光滑，而它那甜美的味道会在舌尖上瞬间融化。

Do you like eating ice cream?

你喜欢吃冰淇淋吗？

Technically, ice cream is a complex frozen dessert.

从技术上讲，冰淇淋是一种复杂的冷冻甜点。

It is made from milk, fresh cream, sugar, and flavorings.

它是由牛奶、鲜奶油、糖和调味料制成的。

It comes in rich colors and delicious tastes.

它色彩丰富，味道鲜美。

While it helps people beat the summer heat, it is primarily loved for bringing joy to children and adults in hot weather.

虽然它能帮助人们抵御夏日炎热，但人们主要还是喜欢它在炎热的天气里给儿童和成人带来欢乐。

Step 1.

第一步。

Prepare the Ice Cream Base.

准备好冰淇淋底料。

Workers mix all raw materials evenly, filter out impurities, homogenize the mixture, and pasteurize it to kill bacteria.

工作人员将所有原材料均匀混合，过滤掉杂质，使混合物均匀化，并对其进行巴氏杀菌以杀死细菌。

After cooling the mixture below 10 degrees Celsius, add fruit flavorings and cool it further to 0–5 degrees Celsius.

将混合物冷却至10摄氏度以下后，加入水果调味料，再进一步冷却至0至5摄氏度。

Step 2.

第二步。

Aging.

陈化。

Put the cooled base into cans and leave it to stand for several hours.

将冷却后的基底装入罐中，静置数小时。

This process greatly improves the smooth texture of ice cream.

这一过程大大提升了冰淇淋的口感顺滑度。

Step 3.

第三步。

Churning and freezing.

翻腾和冻结。

Churn the base continuously while freezing it.

在冷冻的过程中，要不断地搅拌基底。

Air is incorporated into the liquid mixture, turning it into soft, fluffy, semi-solid ice cream.

空气被融入液体混合物中，使其变成柔软蓬松的半固体冰淇淋。

Step 4.

步骤4。

Packaging and hardening.

包装与硬化。

Quickly fill the cups with soft ice cream, then transfer them to a freezer at negative 40 degrees Celsius for deep hardening!

快往杯子里装满软冰淇淋，然后转移到零下40摄氏度的冰箱里进行深度硬化！

Once fully solidified, the finished ice cream is ready.

一旦完全凝固，成品冰淇淋就做好了。
