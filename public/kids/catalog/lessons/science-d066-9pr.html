Where Does Wind Come From?

Where Does Wind Come From?

风是从哪里来的？

Have you ever felt the wind blowing on your face?

你感受过风吹在脸上的感觉吗？

It moves your hair, shakes the trees, and carries kites high up in the sky.

它吹动你的头发，摇晃大树，把风筝高高地送上天空。

But where does wind come from?

但风是从哪里来的呢？

Let's find out!

我们一起来看看吧！

Wind comes from the sun.

风来自太阳。

The sun heats the Earth, but it does not heat everything evenly.

太阳加热地球，但它并不是均匀地加热每一个地方，有些地方会比另一些地方更热。

Some places get hotter than others.

地面附近的空气受热后，会上升飘向天空。

When the air near the ground gets warm, it rises up into the sky.

较冷的空气就涌过来填补空位。

Cooler air rushes in to take its place.

这些流动的空气，就是我们所说的风！

This moving air is what we call wind!

风可以吹得很轻，也可以吹得非常猛。

The wind can blow gently or very strongly.

微风在热天能帮你降温，强风能把大树吹倒，甚至损坏房屋。

A gentle breeze can cool you down on a hot day.

暴风和腿风就是由非常强的风形成的。

A strong windcanblowdown treesand even damagehouses.

风也很有用一它帮助植物传播种子和授粉。

Storms and hurricanes are made of very powerful wind.

人们还利用风能来航行，或者用风力发电机来发电。

Wind is also very useful.

所以下次你感觉到微风拂过脸庞时，记住一一是太阳和空气一起合作，产生了风！

It helps spread seeds and pollinate flowers.

核心单词

People use wind energy to sail boats and make electricity with wind turbines.

人们利用风能来航行船只,用风力涡轮机发电.

So next time you feel the breeze on your face, remember — it is the sun and the air working together to create wind!

所以下次你再感受你脸上的微风,记住——是太阳和空气一起创造风!
