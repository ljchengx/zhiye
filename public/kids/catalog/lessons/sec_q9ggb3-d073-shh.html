Spiders Are Not Insects

English Text

很多人会把蜘蛛误认为昆虫，但其实二者是完全不同的小动物，我们通过数腿就能轻松区分。

Many people mistake spiders for insects, but they are two completely different kinds of small animals.

所有昆虫都长六条腿，而每一只蜘蛛都拥有八条细长的腿，蜘蛛的身体结构也和昆虫不同，并且没有翅膀。

We can tell them apart easily by counting their legs.

大多数蜘蛛可以从身体里吐出细丝，在树枝间、墙壁上编织出细密又有粘性的蛛网。

All insects have six legs, but every spider has eight leng thin legs.

当小飞虫撞上蛛网，就会被牢牢粘住，随后蜘蛛便会前来捕食。

Spiders also have special bodies and no wings at all.

不属于昆虫本课：吐丝结网，依靠蛛网捕猎 英译中：web

Most spiders can produce this silk from their bodies.

They weave beautiful and sticky webs between branches or walls.

When small flying bugs fly into the web, they get stuck.

Then the spider will come out and catch them for food.
