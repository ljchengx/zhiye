General AI Model Deployed on Satellite, News Released Jan 26

General AI Model Deployed onSatellite.

在卫星上部署的通用AI模型。

News Released Jan 26 Chinese space technology firm revealed news on January 26 that the Qwen3 large AI model had been deployed on orbiting satellites in November 2025.

新闻发布 Jan 26 中国航天科技公司于1月26日披露消息, Qwen3大型AI型于2025年11月部署在轨道卫星上.

This is the world's first case of running a general large model in outer space.

这是世界上第一个在外层空间运行一般大型模型的案例.

Ground stations send tasks to satellites, and theAI model completescalculations directly in space before sending results back.

地面站向卫星发送任务, AI模型在将结果送回之前直接在空间完成计算.

The whole process takes less than two minutes.

整个过程用时不到两分钟.

Previously, satellite data had to besent back to Earthfor processing.

以前,卫星数据必须送还地球进行处理。

This breakthrough creates 'space computing satellites".

这个突破创造了"空间计算卫星".

Experts say space-based AI can improve remote sensing, weather observation and disaster monitoring.

专家表示,天基AI可以改进遥感,天气观测和灾害监测.

It opens a new stage for the combination of artificial intelligence and space exploration.

它为人工智能和太空探索的结合开启了一个新的舞台.
