My Goldfish

My Goldfish I have two goldfish at home.

我的金鱼我家里有两条金鱼。

They live in a round fish tank.

它们住在一个圆圆的鱼缸里。

One is orange, and the other is black.

一条是橙色的，另一条是黑色的。

Their tails are like beautiful dresses.

它们的尾巴像漂亮的裙子。

Every morning, I feed them some fish food.

每天早上，我给它们喂鱼食。

They swim fast to the top and open their small mouths.

它们飞快地游到水面，张开小嘴。

After dinner, I like to watch them swimming around.

晚饭后，我喜欢看它们游来游去。

They never close their eyes, even when they sleep.

金鱼连睡觉都不闭眼睛。

Goldfish are my quiet little friends.

它们是我安静的小朋友。
