Why Do We Hiccup?

in Life% Why Do We Hiccup?

中文翻译 为什么会打？

hic!

你有没有突然发出过“”的一声？

Have you ever made a sudden “hic" sound?

那就是打！

That is a hiccup!

有时候很好笑，有时候却很烦人。

It can be funny, but sometimes it is annoying.

我们为什么会打呢？

Why do we hiccup?

来看看吧。

Let's find out.

肺的下面有一块肌肉，叫作“隔肌”。

There is a muscle under your lungs.

这块肌肉帮你呼吸。

It is called the diaphragm.

吸气的时候，它向下拉；呼气的时候，它向上推。

This muscle helps you breathe.

有时候，这块肌肉会闹点小脾气一一它突然抽动一下。

When you breathe in, it pulls down.

这时候，你就会猛地吸一口气。

When you breathe out, it pushes up.

空气快速冲进来，撞到你的声带。

Sometimes, this muscle acts up.

声带赶紧闭上，你就发出了“”的一声。

It twitches or spasms.

打隔有很多原因：吃得太快了、喝了汽水、太兴奋了、太紧张了，甚至笑得太多了！

When that happens, you take in a quick breath.

大多数打很快就停了。

The air rushes in fast and hits your vocal cords.

如果你想让它快点停，可以试试气几秒钟，或者慢慢喝一杯水。

Your vocal cords close quickly, and you make a "hic" sound.

打隔是很正常的。

Hiccups can happen for many reasons.

每个人都会打一一就连妈妈肚子里的小宝宝也会！

Eating too fast.

所以下次你打的时候，等一等就好啦，它很快就走了。

Drinking fizzy drinks.

核心单词

Feeling excited or nervous.

Even laughing too much!

Most hiccups go away quickly.

But if you want to get rid of them, try holding your breath for a few seconds, or drinking a glass of water slowly.
