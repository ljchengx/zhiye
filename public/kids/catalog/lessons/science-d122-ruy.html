Seeds Travel?

RIDE THE WIND

Haveyoueverblownadandelion and watched its seeds fly away?

Plants cannot walk, but their seeds can travel far and wide.

Some seeds ride the wind.

Dandelion seedshave softwhiteparachutes that carry them through the air.

Some seeds catch a ride on animals.

Burrs have tiny hooks that catch onto fur or clothes and drop off somewhere new.

CATCH A RIDE ON ANIMALS

FLOAT ON WATER

Some seeds float on water.

Coconuts drift across the sea on their thick shells that help them float.

Some seeds pop and shoot away.

When a seed pod dries, it bursts and scatters seeds around.

In these clever ways, seeds find new homes and grow into new plants.

CLEVER AND AMAZING!

Seeds are tiny, but they are super travelers.
