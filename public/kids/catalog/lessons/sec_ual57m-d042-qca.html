How Much Is It?

多少钱？

I am at a toy shop with my dad.

我和爸爸在一家玩具店里。

I see a lovely toy bear in the window.

我在橱窗里看到一只可爱的玩具熊。

I ask the shopkeeper, "How much is it?" "It is thirty yuan," she answers.

我问店主：“多少钱？

Dad looks at the price and smiles.

“她回答："三十元。

"It is your birthday gift," he says.

”爸爸看了看价格，笑着说：“这就是你的生日礼物。

I am so happy!

”我太开心了！

I hold the bear and say thank you to the shopkeeper.

我抱着小熊，向店主道谢。

The bear is soft and brown.

小熊软软的、棕色的。

I will name it Coco and take good care of it.

我要给它取名叫可可，好好爱护它。
