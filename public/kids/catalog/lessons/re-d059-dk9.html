The Yellow River - Mother River of China

The Yellow River

Mother River of China Long long ago, there was a great river in China.

Its name is the Yellow River.

People call it "Mother River".

Why?

Because thousands of years ago, many people lived near theYellow River.

They grew rice and wheat.

They built homes.

They told storiesand madebeautiful art.

This is the start of Chinese civilization.

The Yellow River gets its name from the yellow mud in the water.

The mud comes from the Loess Plateau.

When rain washes the soil into the river, the water turnsyellow.

The Yellow River is helpful, but sometimes it brings big floods.
