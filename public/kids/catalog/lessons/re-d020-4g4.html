Qingming Festival

Qingming

清明节通常在4月4日或5日到来。

Festival

这是一个特别的春天时节，人们在这一天缅怀祖先，同时享受大自然的美景。

Qingming Festival, also called Tomb-Sweeping Day, usually falls on April 4th or 5th.

这一天，家人们会去探望亲人的墓地。

It is a special time in spring when people remember their ancestors and enjoy the beauty of nature.

他们清扫墓碑、拔除杂草，并放上鲜花。

On this day, families visit the graves of their loved ones.

许多人还会点香、供奉食物，以表达敬意。

They clean the tombs, remove

这是一个安静而有意义的时刻，让我们感谢先辈。

weeds, and place fresh flowers.

Many people also burn incense and offer food to show respect.

It is a quiet and meaningful moment tosaythankyou tothosewhocamebeforeus.

Qingming is not only a sad day.

It is also a happy spring festival.

The weather is warm, and trees turn green.

People go on outings with friends and family.

They fly kites, take walks in the countryside, and enjoy the fresh air.

This activity is called "taqing" — stepping on the green grass.

A famous food for Qingming is "qingtuan, a green rice ball.

It is made with glutinous rice and a sweet filling, and its green color comes from wormwood juice.

Qingtuan is soft, sweet, and perfect for a spring picnic.
