I Like Drawing

Drawing is my favourite hobby.

我喜欢画画

I have many crayons and brushes.

画画是我最喜欢的爱好。

After school, I often draw in my room.

我有许多蜡笔和画笔。

I draw the blue sky, green trees and cute animals.

放学后，我常常在房间里画画。

My best picture is a big rainbow after the rain.

我画蓝蓝的天空、绿绿的树和可爱的动物。

My art teacher says my pictures are beautiful.

我最满意的一幅画是雨后的大彩虹。

She puts them on the classroom wall.

美术老师说我的画很漂亮，她把它们贴在教室的墙上。

I feel very proud.

我感到非常自豪。

I want to be an artist one day.

我想有一天成为一名画家。
