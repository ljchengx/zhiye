How Is Bread Made?

in Life

面包是怎么做出来的？

How Bread Made?

英文 音标 中文

Is

你喜欢新鲜面包吗？

Do you like fresh bread?

闻起来好香！

It smells so good!

但面包是怎么做出来的呢？

But how is bread made?

我们来看看！

Let's find out!

面包需要面粉、水、酵母、盐和黄油。

Bread needs flour, water, yeast, salt, and butter.

黄油让面包又软又香。

Butter makes the bread soft and yummy.

酵母非常小，你看不见它，但它很重要！

Yeast is very tiny.

把面粉和水混合，面粉变得有弹性，这叫面筋。

You cannot see it, but it is important!

把酵母放进温水里，它就醒了，开始产生小气泡。

Mix flour and water.

这叫发酵。

The flour gets stretchy.

把所有材料混合，得到软软的面团。

This is called gluten.

揉面团，推一推、叠一叠，重复很多次。

Add yeast to warm water.

让面团休息一下。

It wakes up and makes tiny bubbles.

酵母继续产生气泡，面团越来越大。

This is called fermentation.

整好形放进烤箱。

Mix everything together.

高温把面包烤熟，变成金黄色。

You get soft dough.

真好吃！

Knead the dough.

Push and fold it many times.

Let the dough rest.

The yeast keeps making bubbles.

The dough grows bigger and bigger.

Shape the dough and put it in the oven.

The heat bakes the bread.

It turns golden brown.

Yum!
