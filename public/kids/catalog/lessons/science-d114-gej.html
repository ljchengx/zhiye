Self-Driving Cars

Self-Driving Cars With the rapid development of modern technology, self-drivingcarshavegraduallystepped intoourdaily life.

随着现代科技的飞速发展，无人驾驶汽车逐渐走进我们的生活，是新时代最了不起的科技发明之一。

They are among the most amazing inventions in the new technological age.

和传统汽车不同，无人驾驶汽车不需要人类操控。

Different from traditional cars, self-driving cars do notneed humandrivers tocontrol them.

它依靠人工智能、传感器和大数据平稳安全运行。

Theydepend on artificial intelligence, sensors and big data to work safelyandsmoothly.

这些智能设备能让汽车“看清”路面、识别红绿灯与路标，及时避开障碍物。

Thesesmartdevicescanhelp the car“see”theroad clearly, recognize trafficlights and road signs, and avoid obstacles in time.

人类开车时容易疲劳、分心，从而引发交通事故。

While people are driving ordinary cars, they may feel tired or get careless, which may cause traffic accidents.

但无人驾驶汽车可以始终保持专注，精准控制车速和方向、遵守交通规则，大幅降低事故风险，让出行更安全。

However, self-driving cars can always keep focused on the road.

无人驾驶科技也极大便利了生活。

They can control speed and directionaccuratelyandfollowtrafficrulesall thetime.

旅途当中，乘客可以休息、阅读、听音乐，无需一直紧盯路况，节省时间和精力。

In this way, they greatly reduce the risk of traffic accidents and makeour tripsmuchsafer.

同时，这项技术让城市交通更有序、高效。

Self-driving technology also brings us great convenience.

如今越来越多城市开始测试和使用无人驾驶汽车。

It can save people's time and energy during the journey.

虽然这项技术仍在完善，但它充分展现了科技创新的力量。

For example, passengers can rest, read or enjoy music instead of watching theroad all thetime.

相信随着科技不断进步，未来无人驾驶会更加普及，更好地服务大众。

Atthesame time, thissmarttechnologymakescitytrafficmoreorderly and efficient.

我们相信,随着不断的技术进步,自驾汽车将变得更加受欢迎,并在未来为更多的人服务。
