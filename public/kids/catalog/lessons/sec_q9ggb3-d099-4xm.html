Heat and Temperature

Temperature Heat and

Heat is a kind of energy, and temperature tells us how hot or cold something is.

We can feel high temperature as hot, and low temperature as cold.

Heat always moves from hot things to cold things.

If you touch a warm cup, heat will transfer to your cold hands and make your hands warm.

The Sun sends great heat to the Earth and changes our daily temperature.

Different seasons also have different temperatures, which change our life and nature a lot.
