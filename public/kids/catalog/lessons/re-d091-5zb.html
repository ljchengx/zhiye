Kongming Lanterns: The First Hot Air Balloons

Lanterns: Kongming The First Hot Air Balloons

万事如意 心想事成

Haveyou ever seen a glowing paper lantern floating up into the night sky?

孔明灯：最早的热气球

That is a Kongming lantern, also called a sky lantern.

你见过夜晚发光的纸灯笼缓升上天空吗？

Itis anancient Chineseinvention thatworks like a small hot airballoon.

那就是孔明灯，也叫天灯。

Kongminglanterns aremade of thinpaper stretched over a bamboo frame.

它是一项古老的中国发明，工作理就像一个小型热气球。

Atthe bottom, there is a small candle or fuel block.

孔明灯由薄纸和竹制框制成。

When you light the candle, it heats the air inside thelantern.

底部放着一小根烛成燃料块。

Hotairislighter than cold air, so thelantern risesup into the sky!

点燃烛后，灯内的空气被加热。

The name"Kongming"comes from Zhuge Liang, a famousmilitaryleaderfrom the Three Kingdoms period.

热空气比冷空气经，所以孔明灯会慢慢升上天空！

His title was Kongming.

“孔明”这个名字来自三国时期的著名军事家诸离亮，他的名号叫“孔明”。

People say he used these lanterns tosend signals duringbattles.

传说他管用孔明灯在战斗中传避信号。

In ancient China, they were used for military communication.

在古代中国，孔明灯被用于军事通信。

Soldierscouldseethe lanterns fromfar away at night.

士兵们在夜间从远处就能看到这些灯笼。

Today, people release Kongming lanterns duringfestivals.

如今，人们在节日里放飞孔明灯。

Theywritetheirwishes on the lanterns before sending them up.

放飞前，他们会在灯上写下自己的心愿。

It is a way to hope for good luck and happiness.

这是一种祈求好运和幸福的方式。
