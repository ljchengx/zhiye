English Reading

英文阅读

Long ago in China, there was a musician named Yu Boya.

He played the guqin, a traditional Chinese instrument.

One day he met a woodcutter called Zhong Ziqi.

Ziqi understood Boya's musicwithout a singleword.

WhenBoya thought of high mountains, Ziqi said, “I see the tall mountains." When Boya thought of flowing water, Ziqi said, “I hear the running river." They became true friends.

They met every year to play and listen.

Later, Ziqi died.

Boya was very sad.

He broke his guqin and never played again.

He lost the only friend who really understood him.

This story teaches us:

across theworld.

中文翻译

懂你的心 真正的朋友

很久以前，中国有一位叫俞伯牙的琴师。

他弹奏古琴，一种中国传统乐器。

一天，他遇见一位叫钟子期的樵夫。

子期不用一个字就能听懂伯牙的音乐。

当伯牙想到高山，子期说：“我看到了高高的山峰。

”当伯牙想到流水，子期说：“我听到了奔流的河水。

”他们成了真正的朋友。

每年都相聚弹琴、听琴。

后来，子期去世了。

伯牙非常伤心。

他摔碎了古琴，从此不再弹奏。

他失去了唯一真正懂他的人。

这个故事告诉我们：真正的朋友，懂你的心。
