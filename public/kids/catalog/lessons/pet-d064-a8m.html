THEGLOBALCHRONICLE Tesla Unveils New Roadster: Can It ReallyFly?

n the night of October 15, 2026, Tesla unveiled its long-awaited updated Roadster at a SpaceX test facility in Texas.

Elon Musk said the redesigned car combined extreme road speed with advanced aerial mobility.

During the show, an Optimus robot sat in the driver's seat instead of a human.

The car climbed a steep ramp, flipped midair, and hovered briefly above a row of parked cars using built-in cold-gas thrusters.

Tesla claims its top version can accelerate to 6o mph in under 0.59 seconds.

However, there is an important catch.

The version with the flying system is unlikely to be legal on public roads.

The version that can be driven on streets does not have the thrusters.

Despite the impressive show, industry observers remain doubtful.

The car did not achieve stable, sustained flight; its aerial movements were only short, controlled hops.
