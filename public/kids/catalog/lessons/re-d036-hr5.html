What Was the Three Kingdoms Period?

Read and Learn What Was the Three Kingdoms s Period?

The Three Kingdoms period is one of the most famous times in Chinese history.

It lasted from about the year 220 to 280, after the big Han Dynasty fell apart.

China was split into three kingdoms: Wei in the north, Shu in the southwest, and Wu in the 蜀 southeast.

This was a time of great wars, clever plans, and brave heroes.

The most well-known story is about the three sworn brothers: Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei.

They promised to protect each other and bring peace to the land.

Liu Bei was kind and caring, Guan Yu was brave and loyal, and Zhang Fei was strong and fearless.

Together, they built the Shu Kingdom with the help of the brilliant advisor Zhuge Liang.

Zhuge Liang, also known as Kongming, is one of the smartest people in Chinese history.

He was a great strategistwho couldpredict theweather and outsmart his enemies.

Stories say he once borrowed arrows from the enemy using straw boats and fog!

His loyalty to Liu Bei and Shu Kingdom is still admired today.

The Three Kingdoms story has been told for hundreds of years in books, operas, games, and TV shows.

The famous novel Romance of the Three Kingdoms is one of China's four greatest classic books.

People still talk about the heroes and their adventures.

The stories teach us about friendship, wisdom, and never giving up.
