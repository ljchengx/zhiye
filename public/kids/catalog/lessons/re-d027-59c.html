Double NinthFestival?

重阳节在农历九月初九这一天。

The Double NinthFestival, also called Chongyang Festival,falls on the ninth day of the ninth lunar month.

很久以前人们认为数字九力量很强，两个九叠在一起可能会带来坏运气。

Long ago, people believed that the number nine wasvery strong,and twoninestogether could bringbad luck.

为了平安，家家户户会登高山，还会佩戴茱英。

Tostaysafe,familieswould climb high mountains and wear a special plant called zhuyu.

有一个著名的故事讲的是桓景。

Onefamous story tells of aman named HuanJing.

一位智者警告他村里将有灾难。

Awise teacherwarnedhim that a disasterwouldcometohisvillage.

桓景带着家人登上高山，等他们回来时发现村庄躲过了灾祸。

HuanJing took his family up a mountain, and when they returned, theyfound that everyonewassafe and thevillage had been spared from harm.

从那时起登高就成了躲避危险、祈求平安的方式。

From that day on, climbing high became a way to avoid danger and pray for peace.

如今人们通过远足、赏菊花和喝菊花酒来庆祝。

Today, people celebrate by going on hikes, enjoying chrysanthemum flowers, and drinking chrysanthemumwine.

菊花象征长寿，金黄色的花瓣让人想起秋天的美丽。

The chrysanthemumisa symbol oflonglife,andthebrightyellowpetals remindpeople ofautumn's beauty.

现在重阳节也叫老年节，孩子们会去看望爷爷奶奶，送上礼物。

Somefamilies alsowearzhuyubranchesontheirclothes.

重阳节教会我们感恩，也提醒我们关爱身边的长辈。

Nowadays, the festival has a new meaning: showing love and respect for the elderly.

In China, it is also known asSenior Citizens'Day.

Childrenvisit their grandparents, bring them gifts, and spend the day together.

TheDoubleNinthFestival teachesusto be thankful and to care for the older people in our lives.
