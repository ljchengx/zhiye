Dolphins Smart Animals

English Text

海豚可爱又聪明，对人类十分友善。

Dolphins are lovely and smart sea animals.

海豚喜欢跃出水面、结伴嬉戏，还能学会很多简单的动作。

They are very friendly to people.

海豚并不是鱼类，而是海洋哺乳动物，需要定时浮出水面呼吸空气。

Dolphins like to jump out of the water and play together.

They can learn many easy tricks.

They are not fish.

Dolphins are sea mammals and need to come up for air sometimes.
