Hua Mulan: A Brave Girl in Chinese History

Chinese History Hua Mulan: A Brave Girl in

Hua Mulan is a famous girl in ancient China.

Her storyhasbeentoldformorethan1,500years.

Mulan livedwithher oldfather, hermother, andher younger sister.

One day, the emperor asked every family to send one man to fight in the war.

But Mulan's father was too old and sick to go.

Mulan loved her father very much.

She decided to take his place.

She cut her long hair, put on her father's armor, androde ahorse tojoin the army.

For twelve years, she fought bravely in many battles.

No one knew she was a girl.

She became a great warrior and helped her country win the war.

When the war ended, Mulan returned home.

Her old friends were surprised to see her as a woman.

But they all respected her.

Mulan's story teaches us that courage and love for family are more important than being strong or being a boy.

Today, Mulan is known all over the world.

Disney madeacartoonmovieaboutherin1998, and a live-actionmoviein2020.

Many childrenloveher because she is smart, brave, and kind.
