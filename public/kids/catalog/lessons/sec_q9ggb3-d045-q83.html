Rivers and Streams

Rivers and streams are running water on land.

河流与小溪是陆地上流动的水体，小溪比河流更细小。

A stream is smaller than a river.

水总是从高处流向低处。

Water flows from high places to low places.

大多数河流发源于高山，一路奔向大海或湖泊。

Most rivers start in mountains and run towards the sea or lakes.

河流中生活着鱼类和水生植物，人们也利用河水生活、灌溉农田。

Many fish and water plants live in rivers.

规律：水由高处流向低处作用：孕育水生生物，供给人类用水

People also use river water for daily life and farming.
