Gravity

Key Words Page 26 Gravity Gravity pulls everything down.

重力是地球上一种看不见的力，无处不在。

Gravityis an invisible force on Earth,and it exists everywhere around us.

地球的引力会把所有物体拉向地面；这就是为什么向上抛球，球总会落回地面，我们也能稳稳站立，不会飘向空中。

It is the Earth's gravity that pulls all things toward the ground.

太空中所有大型天体都拥有自身引力，天体体积越大，引力就越强。

That is why a ball will fall down when you throw it up, and we can stand firmly on the land instead of floating away.

太阳强大的引力，将八大行星和其他天体牢牢固定在轨道上。

Every big object in space has its own gravity.

mg 在远离大型行星的外太空，引力会变得极其微弱，人和物品就会自由漂浮。

The bigger the object is,the stronger its pulling force.

引力是自然界最基础、最重要的力量之一。

The Sun's strong gravity holds all its eight planets and other celestial bodies in their orbits.

In outer space far away from large planets, gravity becomes very weak, so people and objects will floatfreely.

Gravity is one of the most basic and important forces in nature.
