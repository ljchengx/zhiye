Mammals Animals With Fur

English Text

哺乳动物身体大多覆盖毛发，属于恒温动物。

Mammals usually have fur or hair on their bodies.

Knowledge Summary绝大多数哺乳动物不产卵，母兽会用乳汁喂育幼崽。

They are warm-blooded animals.

特征：体表覆毛、恒温、胎生、哺乳狗、猫、大象还有人类，都属于哺乳动物。

Most mammals do not lay eggs.

举例：猫狗、大象、人类

Mother mammals feed their babies with milk.

Dogs, cats, elephants and humans are all mammals.
