Our Classroom

This is our classroom.

我们的教室

It is big and bright.

这是我们的教室。

There are forty

它又大又明亮。

desks and chairs in it.

教室里有四十套桌椅。

A big blackboard is on the wall.

墙上有一块大黑板。

Near the window, there are beautiful pictures.

窗户旁边挂着美丽的图画。

We read books and write here every day.

我们每天在这里读书、写字。

After class, we clean the classroom together.

下课后，我们一起打扫教室。

I like my classroom.

我喜欢我的教室。

It is our happy home at school.

它是我们在学校快乐的家。
