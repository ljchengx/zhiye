Why do peacocks spread their tails?

Spread Their Tails?

Why Do Peacocks

in Life

Haveyou everseen apeacockspread its huge, colorful tail?

It looks like a beautiful fan!

But why do they do that?

First, only male peacocks spread their tails.

They do this to attract female peacocks.

The bigger and brighter the tail,the more the femaleslike it.

It is like saying, “Look at me!

I am strong and healthy!"

Second, peacocks spread their tails to scare away enemies.

When they shake their feathers, the many “eye" spots on the tail move.

This can make other animals feel frightened and run away.

So, a peacock's tail is not just for show - it helps them find a mate and stay safe!

中文翻译

孔雀为什么要开屏？

你见过孔雀展开它那巨大又五彩缤纷的尾巴吗？

看起来像一把美丽的扇子！

但它们为什么要这样做呢？

首先，只有雄性孔雀才会开屏。

它们这样做是为了吸引雌性孔雀。

尾巴越大越鲜艳，雌性就越喜欢。

这就像在说：“快看我！

我又强壮又健康！

”

其次，孔雀开屏是为了吓跑敌人。

当它们抖动羽毛时，尾巴上许多“眼睛”形状的花纹会跟着动，这会让其他动物感到害怕而逃走。

所以，孔雀的尾巴不只是好看而已一一它能帮助孔雀找到伴侣，还能保护自己的安全！
