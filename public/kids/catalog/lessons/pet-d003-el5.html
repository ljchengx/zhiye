China Launches New Home & Digital Goods Replacement Subsidy（Early Jan）

China a Launches New

Home & Digital Goods Replacement Subsidy At the start of 2026, China rolled out a new

subsidy policy for home appliances, mobile phones and digital products trade-in.

The policy encourages citizens to replace old equipment with new energy-saving and smart goods.

Consumers can get up to 15 percent subsidies with a maximum allowance of 1,500 yuan.

Local governments and retailers have launched matching promotions to boost domestic consumption.

Officials believe the policy can stimulate market demand and push industrial upgrading.

Industry insiders expect the scheme to drive billions of yuan in sales in the first quarter.

It also helps low-carbon development, because new electronics and home appliances consume less electricity and produce fewer pollutants.
