Why Do Leaves Change Color?

你知道为什么秋天树叶会从绿色变成黄色、橙色或红色吗？

Do you knowwhy tree leaves turn from green to yellow, orange, or red in autumn?

这不是魔法一一这是科学！

It is not magic —— it is science!

在春天和夏天，叶子是绿色的，因为它们含有大量的叶绿素。

In spring and summer, leaves are green because they have a lot of chlorophyll.

叶绿素帮助叶子利用阳光为树木制造养分。

Chlorophyll helps leaves use sunlight to make food for the tree.

叶绿素是绿色的，所以叶子看起来也是绿色的。

It is green, so it makes the leaves look green.

但是当秋天来临时，白天变短了，天气也变凉了。

But when autumn comes, the days get shorter and theweathergets cooler.

树木知道冬天要来了，所以它们停止制造新的叶绿素。

Treesknowthatwinteris coming, so they stop making new chlorophyll.

旧的叶绿素分解并消失。

The old chlorophyll breaks down and goes away.

然后，原本藏在叶子里的其他颜色就开始显现出来了！

Then, Spring&Summer Autumn othercolorsthatwerehiddenintheleavesstartto show!

这些颜色就是黄色、橙色和红色。

These colors are yellow, orange, and red.

所以，秋天美丽的颜色其实一直都在叶子里一一只是我们要等到绿色的叶绿素消失后才能看到它们！

So, thebeautiful autumncolorsarealwaystherein theleaves—wejust cannot see them until the green chlorophyll disappears!
