Chinese Paper Cutting

Paper Chinese Cutting

Chinese paper cutting is a traditional folk art with a history of over 1,500 years.

It started in the Han Dynasty and became popular during the Tang and Song Dynasties.

People use scissors or knives to cut paper into beautiful patterns.

To make a paper cutting, you first fold the red paper.

Then you draw a simple design on it.

After that, you carefully cut along the lines.

When you open the paper, a wonderful picture appears.

Red is the most common color because it means happiness and good luck in China.

The patterns often come from nature and daily life.
