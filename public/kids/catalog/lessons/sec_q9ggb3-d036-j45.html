Chinese Paper Cutting

Read and Learn Chinese Paper Cutting Chinese paper cutting is a traditional folk art with a history of over 1,500 years.

读学中国剪纸中国是民俗艺术,历史长达1500多年.

It started in the Han Dynasty and became popular during the Tang and Song Dynasties.

始于汉朝,在唐,宋两朝时期流行.

People use scissors or knives to cut paper into beautiful patterns.

人们使用剪刀或刀将纸张切成美丽的图案.

To make a paper cutting, you first fold the red paper.

为了剪纸,你先把红纸叠起来

Then you draw a simple design on it.

然后你在上面画了一个简单的设计.

After that, you carefully cut along the lines.

在那之后,你小心地切线。

When you open the paper, a wonderful picture appears.

当你打开报纸时, 出现了一幅很棒的照片。

Red is the most common color because it means happiness and good luck in China.

红色是最常见的颜色,因为它意味着幸福和好运在中国.

The patterns often come from nature and daily life.

这些模式往往来自自然和日常生活。

You can see flowers, birds, fish, and Chinese characters like "福" (good fortune).

可以看到花,鸟,鱼,以及"Wo"(好运气)等中国人物.

Animals like dragons and phoenixes stand for power and peace.

龙凤等动物代表力量与和平.

Many people put paper cuttings on windows, doors, and walls during the Spring Festival.

许多人在春节期间在窗户,门和墙上都贴上剪纸的纸.

They believe the art brings joy and drives away bad luck.

他们相信艺术能带来欢乐 并驱赶厄运

Today, paper cutting is not only a hobby for old people.

今天,剪纸不仅是老人的爱好.

Children and young people also love it.

儿童和青年人也喜欢它。

It is a great way to show Chinese culture and make our lives more colorful.

这是展示中国文化,使我们的生活更加多彩的伟大方法.
