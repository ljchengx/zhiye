EU Sets Strict Greenhouse Gas Cut Target for 2040

EU SetsStrict Greenhouse Gas Cut Target for 2040 On March 5, the European Union formally passed updated European ClimateLaw.

3月5日,欧盟正式通过了更新的《欧洲气候法》。

ItrequiresEUmembers to cut netgreenhousegas emissions by90percentcomparedwiththe level in 1990 by the year 2040.

要求欧盟成员国在2040年之前将温室气体净排放量与1990年的水平相比减少90%。

To reach the big goal, European countriesneed to speed up theuse of wind, solar and other clean energy.

为了达到这个大目标,欧洲国家需要加快风能,太阳能和其他清洁能源的利用.

Traditional high-polluting industries will carry out large-scale low-carbon transformation.

传统高污染产业将开展大规模低碳转型.

Many new policies about green transport and energy saving will come out step by step.

许多关于绿色运输和节能的新政策将逐步出台。

Environmental Iactivistswelcomethenewrule.

环境活动家欢迎新规则。

However, somebusiness groups worry the tight target may bring pressure on production costs.

然而,一些商业团体担心目标过于严格可能会给生产成本带来压力。

Governments will keep looking for balanced ways to protect both the environment and economic growth.

各国政府将不断寻找保护环境和经济增长的平衡方法。
