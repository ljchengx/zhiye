Deep Sea Dark World

English Text

深海科普

The deep sea is far below the sea surface.

深海位于海面以下极深的区域，阳光无法抵达，这里终年漆黑。

Sunlight cannot reach here, so it is dark all the time.

深海环境寒冷，水压巨大，只有外形奇特的特殊生物才能在此生存。

It is very cold and has strong water pressure.

部分深海生物可以自身发光，用来视物和觅食。

Only special strange animals can live in the deep sea.

Some deep-sea animals make their own light to see and COLD find food.
