The Forbidden City

The Forbidden City TheForbidden Cityis afamouspalacein the center of Beijing, China.

故宫是中国北京市中心一座著名的宫殿。

It was built about 600years ago during the Ming Dynasty.

它大约建于六百年前的明朝。

The name "Forbidden"means ordinary people could not enter it in the past.

“紫禁城”的意思是过去普通人不能进入这里。

Today, it is also called thePalaceMuseum.

如今它也被称为故宫博物院。

TheForbidden City isvery large.

故宫非常大，有九千多个房间！

It hasmore than 9,000 rooms!

建筑有黄色的屋顶和红色的墙壁。

The buildings have yellow roofsandredwalls.

黄色是皇帝的颜色，红色代表好运。

Yellowwasthecolor of the emperor, and red meant good luck.

屋顶是弯曲的，墙壁上装饰着美丽的绘画和雕刻。

The roofsarecurved, and thewallsaredecorated with beautiful paintings and carvings.

它是中国古代建筑最杰出的代表之一。

It is oneof thebestexamplesof ancient Chinese architecture.

明清两代的二十四位皇帝曾在这里生活和工作。

For almost 500 years,24 emperors of the Ming and Qing Dynasties lived and worked there.

现在故宫是一座博物馆，每天都有来自世界各地的人来参观。

They held meetings, celebrated festivals, and made important decisions in these halls.

1987年它被列为联合国教科文组织世界逮产。

The emperors' families also lived in the inner courtyards.

Now, the Forbidden City is a museum.

People from all over the world visit it every day.

They can see ancient treasures, paintings, and furniture.

In 1987, it became a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Itis awonderfulplace tolearn about Chinese history.
