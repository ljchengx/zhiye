Tai Chi: A Moving Meditation

Read and Learn Tai Chi: A Moving Meditation Tai Chi, also called Taijiquan, is an ancient Chinese martial art.

It started hundreds of years ago in China.

People first practiced it for self-defense, but today many do it for health and relaxation.

The most important idea in Tai Chi is yin and yang.

Yin and yang are two opposite forces thatbalance each other, like day and night, or soft and hard.

In Tai Chi, you move slowly and smoothly in circles.

You breathe deeply and keep your body relaxed.

This helps your mind stay calm and focused.

Tai Chi is very good for your health.

It makes your muscles stronger and your balance better.

It can also reduce stress and help you sleep well.

Because the movements are gentle, even old people and children can do it safely.

Many schools in China teach Tai Chi to students during breaks.

Today, Tai Chi is popular all over the world.

People in parks in New York, London, and Tokyo practice it every morning.

It is a beautiful way to exercise both the body and the mind.

Why not try it yourself?
