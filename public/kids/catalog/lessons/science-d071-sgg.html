But where does it come from?

巧克力来自一种叫“可可果”的水果。

It does not grow in a factory.

可可果长在可可树上，可可树生长在赤道附近温暖多雨的地方。

It grows on trees!

可可果大大的，有黄色、红色或紫色。

Chocolate comes from a fruit called the cacao pod.

每个果荚里大约有30到50粒种子，这些种子就叫“可可豆”。

It grows on the cacao tree in warm, rainy places near the equator.

要做出巧克力，人们先采摘可可果，取出里面的可可豆。

The pods are big and colorful—- yellow, red, or purple.

然后把豆子晒干、烘烤。

Inside each pod, there are about 30 to 50 seeds.

烤好之后，把豆子

These seeds are called cacao beans.

压碎，变成一种浓稠的糊状物，叫作“可可浆”。

To make chocolate, people pick the pods and take out the beans.

可可浆加上糖和其他配料，就做成了我们吃的巧克力。

Then they let the beans dry and roast them.

黑巧克力不加牛奶，味道更浓更苦。

After roasting, the beans are crushed into a thick paste called chocolate liquor.

牛奶巧克力加了牛奶，更甜更滑。

White chocolate has no cocoa powder, only cocoa butter, so it is white.

白巧克力没有可可粉,只有可可黄油,所以是白色的.

Some chocolates also have nuts or dried fruit inside.

一些巧克力内部也有坚果或干果.
