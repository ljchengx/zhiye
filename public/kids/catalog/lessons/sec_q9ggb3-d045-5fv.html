Why Do the Mogao Caves Shine Like Stars in the Desert?

Read and Learn Why Do the Mogao Caves s Shine Like Stars in the Desert?

阅读和学习为什么摩高洞像沙漠中的星星一样闪耀?

Have you ever seen an art museum hidden inside a mountain?

你见过藏在山里的艺术博物馆吗?

The Mogao Caves, also called the “Caves of a Thousand Buddhas,” are a group of ancient caves in Dunhuang, Gansu Province, China.

摩诃洞窟,又称"千佛洞窟",是中国甘肃省敦煌市的一个古洞窟群.

They are one of the four greatest grottoes in China and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

他们是中国四大古迹之一,也是联合国教科文组织世界遗产.

For over a thousand years, monks and artists carved and painted these caves.

千余年来,僧侣和艺术家雕刻和绘画了这些洞穴.

Inside the caves, you can find thousands of colorful Buddhist murals and huge statues.

在洞穴内部,可以找到数千幅彩色的佛教壁画和巨大的雕像.

The most famous images are the “Flying Apsaras" — beautiful heavenly dancers with flowing ribbons.

最著名的图像是"飞翔的阿普拉斯"(Flying Apsaras)——美丽的天舞者,带有流淌的丝带.

These paintings show stories from Buddhist teachings, daily life, and even trade along the Silk Road.

这些绘画展现了佛教教义,日常生活,甚至丝绸之路沿线贸易的故事.

That is why people call the Mogao Caves a treasure house of art and history.

因此人们称摩诃迦叶窟为"艺术与历史的宝库".

Here is a fun fact: In 1900, a monk named Wang Yuanlu discovered a hidden library cave filled with over 50,000 ancient manuscripts.

这里有一个有趣的事实:1900年,一个名叫王元鲁的僧侣发现了一个藏有5万多件古代手稿的藏书洞.

The documents included books, letters, and paintings in many languages.

这些文件包括多种语言的书籍、信件和绘画。

This discovery amazed the world and helped scholars understand ancient cultures better.

这一发现令世界大吃一惊,帮助学者更好地理解古代文化.

Today, scientists use digital technology to photograph every cave, so everyone can explore them online.

今天,科学家们利用数字技术拍摄每个洞穴,这样每个人都可以在网上探索.

The Mogao Caves are not just old stones; they are a bridge between the past and the future.

莫高窟不仅是旧石器,是过去与未来的桥梁.

They remind us how people from different lands shared ideas and beauty.

它们提醒我们,来自不同土地的人如何分享思想和美。

By protecting these caves, we keep a bright star of human creativity shining forever.

通过保护这些洞穴,我们让人类创造力的明亮之星永远闪耀。
