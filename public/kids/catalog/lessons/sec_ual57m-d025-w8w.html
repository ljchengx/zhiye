After School

After School

放学后

School is over at four o'clock.

学校四点放学。

I say goodbye to my teachers and friends.

我向老师和朋友们说再见，然后背着书包回家。

Then I go home with my schoolbag.

首先，我做家庭作业。

First, I do my homework.

然后我和哥哥在院子里踢足球。

Then I play football with my brother in the yard.

六点，我们一起吃饭。

At six, we have dinner together.

晚饭后，我读一本故事书。

After dinner, I read a storybook.

九点，我上床睡觉。

At nine, I go to bed.

多么美好的一天啊！

What a nice day!
