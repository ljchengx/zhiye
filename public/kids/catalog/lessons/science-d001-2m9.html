Now run your finger along the middle of your back.

Those little bumps are

your spine.

Your spine helps you bend over and stand up straight.

Your bones all fit together to make a skeleton.

Each bone has a special metacarpus phalanges name.

Your skull is made up of many

bones.

In fact, if we count all the bones in your head —— including the tiny ones in your ears —— there are 28 in total!

Your bones mostly grow while you sleep.

Doctors can see your bones in an metatarsus phalanges X-ray picture.

That's how they check if your bones are healthy and strong.
