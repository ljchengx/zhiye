Your Bones

Your Bones Touch your head.

摸摸你的头。

Can you feel your skull?

你能摸到你的头骨吗？

Your skull has an important job —it protects your brain.

你的头骨有一个重要的工作一一它保护你的大脑。

Squeeze your hand.

握紧你的手。

Can you feel the bones beneath your skin?

你能摸到皮肤下面的骨头吗？

They are hard like sternum rib rocks, but they are also alive.

它们像石头一样坚硬，但它们也是活的。

They grow as you grow.

它们会随着你的成长而生长。

humerus Now run your finger along the middle of your back.

现在用手指沿着你的背部中间摸下去。

Those little bumps are your spine.

那些小小的突起就是你的脊柱。

Your spine helps you bend over and stand up straight.

你的脊柱帮助你弯腰和挺直站立。

Your bones all fit together to make a skeleton.

你的骨头组合在一起，形成了骨骼。

Each bone has a special metacarpus phalanges name.

每块骨头都有一个特殊的名称。

Your skull is made up of many bones.

你的头骨由许多块骨头组成。

In fact, if we count all the bones in your head —— including the tiny ones in your ears —— there are 28 in total!

事实上，如果把头部所有的骨头都算上，包括耳朵里的小骨头，总共有28块呢！

Your bones mostly grow while you sleep.

你的骨头大部分在你睡觉的时候生长。

Doctors can see your bones in an metatarsus phalanges X-ray picture.

医生可以在X光片中看到你的骨头。

That's how they check if your bones are healthy and strong.

那就是他们检查你的骨骼是否健康和强壮的方式。
