At the Restaurant

At the Restaurant

在餐馆里

Today is Sunday.

今天是星期天。

My family is eating at a restaurant.

我们一家人在餐馆吃饭。

A waiter gives us the menu.

服务员递给我们菜单，上面有许多菜。

There are many dishes on it.

爸爸点了鱼和米饭，妈妈点了一些蔬菜，我想要饺子一一那是我最喜欢的食物。

Dad orders fish and rice.

饭菜很美味。

Mom orders some vegetables.

我们边吃边聊，非常开心。

I want dumplings.

多么美好的周日午餐啊！

They are my favourite food.

The food is delicious.

We eat and talk happily.

What a nice Sunday lunch!
