The Silk Road: A Bridge Between East and West

Read and Learn The Silk Road: ABridgeBetweenEastandWest The Silk Road was not just one road —it was a network of trade routes connecting China to the West.

阅读和学习丝绸之路:ABridgeBetween东西方的丝绸之路不仅仅是一条道路——它是连接中国和西方的贸易路线网.

It started over 2,000 years ago during the Han Dynasty.

始建于汉朝时期,距今2000多年.

The road stretched from Chang'an (today's Xi'an) all theway toRome and otherplaces inEurope.

这条路从长安(今天的西安)一直延伸到罗马和欧洲其他地方.

Itwas one of the longest and most important trade routes in history.

它是历史上最长和最重要的贸易路线之一.

Why was it called theSilkRoad?

为什么叫"西尔克之路"?

Because silk was the most famous product that traveled from China to the West.

因为丝绸是从中国到西方旅行的最著名的产品.

Chinese silk was soft, light, and beautiful.

中国丝绸柔软,轻巧,美丽.

People in Rome and other countries loved it and paid a lot of gold for it.

罗马和其他国家的人们都很喜欢它,为此付出了大量的黄金.

But silk was not the only thing.

但丝绸不是唯一的东西。

Chinese traders also sent tea, porcelain, paper, and spices.

中国商人也派茶,瓷,纸,和香料来.

In return, China got glass, wool, grapes, and Chang'an new ideas from the West.

作为回报,中国有玻璃,羊毛,葡萄,以及来自西方的长安新思想.

(Xi'an) The Silk Road was not just about goods.

(西安)丝绸之路不仅仅是关于商品的.

It was also Rome a road of ideas and culture.

罗马也是一条思想和文化之路。

Buddhism came to China from India along the Silk Road.

佛教从印度沿着丝绸之路传入中国.

Music, art, and stories traveled back and forth.

音乐、艺术和故事不断回溯。

People from different countries met, talked, and learned from each other.

来自不同国家的人相遇,交谈,互相学习.

It was one of the first examples of globalization.

它是全球化的最早例子之一。

Today, the Silk Road is still important.

今天,丝绸之路仍然很重要.

China is building a new "Belt and Road" to connect Asia, Africa, and Europe again.

中国正在建设一条新的"贝尔特与道路",以再次连接亚洲,非洲和欧洲.

The Silk Road shows us that when people share and work together, amazing things can happen.

"丝绸之路"向我们表明,当人们分享和工作时,惊人的事情会发生.

It is a symbol of friendship and exchange between different cultures.

它是不同文化间友谊和交流的象征。
