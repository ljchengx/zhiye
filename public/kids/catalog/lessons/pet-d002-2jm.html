Shenzhou-20 Spacecraft Lands Successfully（Jan 19） English Text

At 9:34 on January 19, 2026, the return capsule of Shenzhou-20 touched down safely at the Dongfeng Landing Site.

北京时间2026年1月19日9时34分，神舟二十号飞船返回舱在东风着陆场平安着陆。

The whole return mission was completed successfully.

返回任务圆满成功。

The spacecraft was launched in April 2025 and stayed in orbit for 270 days.

飞船2025年4月发射，在轨飞行270天。

It returned without astronauts after engineers adjusted the flight plan.

因调整方案，飞船以无人状态返航。

Technicians checked the capsule and confirmed its condition was generally normal.

技术人员现场检查，确认返回舱整体状态正常。

This mission tested the ability of manned spacecraft to stay in orbit for up to nine months.

本次任务验证载人飞船最长9个月在轨停靠能力。

The successful landing marks another important achievement for China's manned space program.

此次顺利返回，是中国载人航天工程又一项重要成果。
