89 Lesson For sale

FOR SALE Good afternoon.

为销售 下午好。

FOR IAN That's right.

对 IAN,没错。

出售尼格尔 我能看看吗?

进来吧

NIGEL How long have you lived here?

尼尔,你住在这里多久了?

IAN I've lived here for twenty years.

我在这里住了20年了

Twenty years!

二十年了! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years! 20 years!

NIGEL That's a long time.

尼格尔,这是很长一段时间。

IAN Yes, I've been here since 1976.

是的,我从1976年开始就在这里。

NIGEL Then why do you want to sell it?

那为什么你要卖掉它?

Because I've just retired.

因为我刚刚退休了。
