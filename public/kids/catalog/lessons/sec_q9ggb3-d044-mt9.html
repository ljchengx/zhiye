Soil and Dirt

English Text

土壤就是植物生长的褐色泥土，里面含有水分和养分。

Soil is the brown ground where plants grow.

青草、鲜花、树木和农作物都离不开土壤。

It has water and nutrients inside.

植物的根扎进泥土里，吸收水分和营养。

All grass, flowers, trees and crops need soil to live.

土壤对陆地上所有生物都至关重要，我们要好好保护土地。

Roots grow down into the soil to drink water and get food.

Soil is very important for all living things on land.

We must take good care of the soil.
