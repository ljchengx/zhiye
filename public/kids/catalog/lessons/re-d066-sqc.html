Confucius and the Love of Learning: China's First Great Teacher

Confucius and the Love of Learning: China's First Great Teacher 孔子与治学 中国第一位伟大的老师 More than 2,000 years ago, a man named Confucius loved to learn.

He read many books and became a great teacher.

Under an apricot tree, he taught anyone whowanted tolearn.

Everyonewhowas eager to study could come to him, rich or poor.

His school had no walls.

He taught them to be kind and to respect others.

He said a famous line: “Learning without thinking is blind; thinking without learning is dangerous."" Many of his students became wise men.

Today, we still remember his spirit of pursuing knowledge.

A good teacher can change the world.
