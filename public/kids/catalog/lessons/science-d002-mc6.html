Why Do We Need to Poop?

We poop to get rid of useless body waste.

我们排便，是为了把身体里没有用的废物排出去。

Our bodies absorb energy and nutrients from food we eat.

身体从吃进去的食物里吸收能量和营养。

Undigested food leftovers become stool and leave the body through the anus.

消化不了的食物残渣会变成粪便，从肛门排出体外。

Irregular bowel movements cause waste to build up in intestines and lead to discomfort.

如果排便不规律，废物会在肠道里堆积，让人不舒服。

Chew food into small pieces in the mouth.

在口腔里把食物嚼碎。

The stomach releases gastric juice to digest food.

胃部分泌胃液来消化食物。

The small intestine absorbs useful nutrients.

小肠吸收有用的营养。

The large intestine absorbs extra water from waste.

大肠从废物里吸收多余的水分。

The whole digestion takes one to two days.

整个消化过程大约需要一到两天。

Smooth bowel movements make us feel relaxed and comfortable.

顺畅的排便会让我们感到轻松、舒服。
