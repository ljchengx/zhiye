Why Do We?

Haveyoueverwonderedwhythesuncomes up in the morning and goes down at night?

Or why summer is hot and winter is cold?

The answerslieinhowEarth moves!

Earth is always moving.

It has two important kinds ofmotion:rotation and revolution.

ROTATION(SPIN) Earth spins on its axis once every~24 hours.

Daytime: Nighttime

Rotation — Earth Spins!

Earth spins around like a giant top.

Ittakesabout24hourstomakeone complete turn.

We call this rotation.

As Earth spins, differentpartsfacethesun.

The side facing the sun has daytime.

The side facing away has nighttime.

That is why we have day and night!

REVOLUTION(ORBIT) Earth orbits the Sun once every~365 days.

Earth's axisis tilted about23.5°.

Revolution-EarthOrbitstheSun!

While Earth is spinning, it is also traveling around the sun.

This journey is called revolution.

It takes one year — about 365 days — to go all the way around.

During this trip, the Earth is slightly tilted.

WhentheNorthPoletiltstoward the sun, it is summer in the north.

When it tilts away, it is winter.

中文翻译

为什么我们有白天和黑夜？

你有没有想过，为什么太阳早上升起、晚上落下？

为什么夏天热、冬天冷？

答案就藏在地球的运动中！

地球一直在运动。

它有两种重要的运动方式：自转和公转。

自转一一地球在旋转！

地球像一个大陀螺一样旋转。

它大约需要24小时转完一圈。

我们称之为自转。

地球旋转时，不同区域会朝向太阳。

面向太阳的一面是白天，背向太阳的一面是黑夜。

这就是为什么我们有白天和黑夜！

公转一一地球绕太阳运行！

地球在自转的同时，也在绕太阳运行。

这段旅程叫作公转。

地球绕太阳一圈需要一年一大约365天。

在这段旅程中，地球是微微倾斜的。

当北极倾向太阳时，北半球就是夏天；当北极背离太阳时，北半球就是冬天。
