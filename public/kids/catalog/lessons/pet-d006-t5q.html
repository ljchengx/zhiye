National Spring Festival Tourism Consumption Month Launched（Jan 29）

National Spring Festival Tourism Consumption Month

Launched (Jan 29)

文化和旅游 全国春节

On January 29,China kicked off the 2026 National Spring Festival Culture and Tourism Consumption Month in Quanzhou.

Over 360 million yuan of consumption couponswill be released nationwide

to boost holiday tourism.

2026年1月29日

From lateJanuary to early March, local authorities will hold around 30,000 cultural and tourism activities.

1月29日，2026年全国春节文化和旅游消费月在福建泉州启动，全国将发放超3.6亿元文旅消费券拉动假日旅游。

Visitors can enjoy ice and snow tours, warm winter trips, folk shows and family-friendly events.

活动从1月底持续至3月初，各地举办约3万场文旅活动，推出冰雪游、避寒游、民俗演出、亲子活动；众多景区实行门票减免、联票优惠政策。

Many scenic spots offer ticket discounts and joint preferential policies.

官方希望通过惠民举措满足民众出行需求，激活文旅市场，同时向海内外游客传播中国传统春节民俗文化。

Officials hope these measures can meet people's travel demands and revive the cultural tourism market.

The activity also helps spread traditional Chinese Spring Festival customs to more tourists at home and abroad.
