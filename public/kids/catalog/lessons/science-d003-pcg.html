Frog Life Cycle

Frog Life Cycle

Frogs are amazing amphibians that go through great changes as they grow.

Their whole life cycle takes aboutfourmonths.

Core Words

amphibian hatch tadpole gill

The Journey of a Frog

On Day 1 female frogs lay thousands of eggs in the water.

All amphibians hatchfrom underwater eggs, and babyfrogscanonly liveinwater at the very beginning.

After twoweeks tadpoles hatch out of the eggs.

Tadpolesliveentirelyinwater and breathe through gills.

They look like tinyfish with long tails.

Threemonths later they turn into froglets.

They grow legs and develop lungs for breathing air.

Now they can live bothinwaterandonland.

By four months old they become fully grown frogs.

They lose their tailscompletely andspendmostof their time on land.

It is amazing to see how amphibians change their bodiesand adapttobothwater andlandstepbystep.

中文翻译

青蛙的生命周期

青蛙是十分神奇的两栖动物，在成长过程中会发生巨大的形态变化，完整的生命周期大约为四个月。

第1天，雌蛙在水中产下成千上万枚蛙卵。

所有两栖动物都从水下的卵孵化而出，蛙的幼体在发育初期只能在水中生存。

三个月时，它们发育成为幼蛙，长出四肢，同时长出肺部用于呼吸空气。

此时它们既可以在水中生活，也能够到陆地上活动。

两周过后，蝌蚪破卵而出。

蝌蚪完全生活在水中，依靠鳃进行呼吸，外形如同拖着长长尾巴的小鱼。

到第四个月，它们就发育为成年青蛙，尾巴完全退化脱落，大部分时间在陆地栖息。

目睹两栖动物一步步完成身体蜕变，逐步适应水陆两种生存环境，是一件非常奇妙的事
