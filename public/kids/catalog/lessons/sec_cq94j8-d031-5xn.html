Lesson 37 Making a bookcase

Making a Bookcase DAN You're working hard, George.

乔治,你正在努力工作

What are you doing?

你们在干什么?

GEORGE I'm making a bookcase.

GEORGE 我正在做一个书架。

GEORGE Give me that hammer please, Dan.

丹,把锤子给我

DAN Which hammer?

哪个锤子?

This one?

这个?

GEORGE No, not that one.

不,不是那个。

The big one.

大的那个。

DAN Here you are.

丹,你在这里。

GEORGE Thanks, Dan.

谢谢,丹。

DAN What are you going to do now, George?

乔治,你现在打算怎么办?

GEORGE I'm going to paint it.

乔治,我要去画它。

DAN What colour are you going to paint it?

你要画什么颜色?

GEORGE I'm going to paint it pink.

我要把它涂成粉红色

PINK DAN Pink!

平克丹粉红!

GEORGE This bookcase isn't for me.

GEORGE 这个书箱不适合我

It's for my daughter, Susan.

给我女儿的,苏珊

Pink's her favourite colour.

粉红色是她最喜欢的颜色
