2026 National Science Popularization Month Kicks Off

Colorful science-related activities are held across the country.

Students visit science museums and botanical gardens.

They observe amazing plant seeds and take part in fun science experiments.

This activity encourages teenagers to get close to science, keep curiosity and build an interest in exploration and innovation.
